Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka CapCut Template 2026

By Prompt Library • 27 June 2026

This sad-mood reel trend is everywhere on Instagram right now. The song “Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka” plays in the background, and the video has this beautiful cinematic look — black and white filter, slow motion, lyrics appearing on screen with a shake effect. It looks like a professional edit, but you can make it in minutes using a CapCut template.

I tested this template myself. The result looks really good, even with a simple video clip. If you want to jump on this trend before it fades, this guide will show you everything — what clip to record, how to use the template, and how to make your reel stand out.

What Does This Template Look Like?

The edit starts with a cinematic opening shot. Then the video plays in slow motion with a black and white filter applied. The song lyrics appear on screen in stylish English text, and a light shake effect gives it that emotional, dramatic feel.

The overall mood is sad and nostalgic. That is why this template works so well with the “Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka” song — the visuals match the feeling of the music perfectly.

What Kind of Video Clip Do You Need?

This is the most important part. The template needs just one video clip from you, and it should have a sad or thoughtful mood.

Here are some simple ideas for what to record:

Option 1 – Easy version: Record yourself walking slowly while looking down or away from the camera. Keep your face natural and a little sad. Even a 5-second clip works.

Option 2 – Cinematic version: Go to an open location — a rooftop, a field, or anywhere with a nice background. Record during the evening when the light is golden. Film from a low angle. Look up at the sky or turn away from the camera. This gives a very filmy, dramatic result.

You do not need a DSLR or any special camera. Your phone camera is enough. Just make sure the location looks clean and the lighting is decent.

How to Use the Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka CapCut Template

Use on CapCut

Follow these steps one by one:

Step 1 – Install CapCut Download the CapCut app from the Play Store or App Store if you do not have it already. It is free.

Step 2 – Open the Template Tap the template button provided on this page. It will open directly inside the CapCut app. If it does not open, turn on a free VPN app on your phone and try again.

Step 3 – Tap “Use Template” You will see a preview of the edit. Tap the “Use Template” button to start.

Step 4 – Add Your Clip Your phone gallery will open. Select the sad-mood video clip you recorded.

Step 5 – Tap Next After selecting your clip, tap the “Next” button. CapCut will automatically apply all the effects — the filter, slow motion, lyrics, and everything else.

Step 6 – Export and Save Once the edit is ready, tap the Export button at the top. Your video will be saved to your gallery.

Step 7 – Post It Upload it as an Instagram Reel or YouTube Short. You can add a caption that matches the sad mood of the song.

Tips to Make Your Reel Look Better

Record your clip in good natural light. Dark or blurry videos will not look good even with the template applied.

Keep your clip steady. If your hands shake while recording, the final video will look messy. Use a surface or a basic tripod to hold your phone.

If you do not like the audio after exporting, open the video in CapCut and replace it with the original song from CapCut’s audio library.

Post your reel early in the morning or in the evening for better reach on Instagram.

What Is a CapCut Template?

If you are new to CapCut, here is a quick explanation. A CapCut template is a pre-made video edit that someone else has already built. All the effects, timing, filters, and music are already set. You just add your own clip, tap next, and your video is ready.

This is why templates are so popular. What would take a skilled editor an hour to build manually, you can do in under 3 minutes using a template. CapCut has thousands of templates for every style — sad, romantic, funny, energetic, and more.

Conclusion

The Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka CapCut template is a great way to make a cinematic, emotional reel without any editing skills. Just record a simple sad-mood clip, use the template link, and your video will be ready in minutes.

Trends like this move fast. If you want views and engagement on Instagram, try this one now while it is still at its peak. The more people use a trend, the harder it becomes to stand out — so the early movers always get the most attention.

FAQ – Tu Pyaar Hai Kisi Or Ka CapCut Template

Q1. Is this template free to use? Yes, it is completely free. You only need the CapCut app installed on your phone.

Q2. The template link is not opening. What should I do? This usually happens because of region restrictions. Download any free VPN app, connect it, and then try the template link again.

Q3. How many video clips does this template need? It needs just one clip — your sad-mood video.

Q4. Can I use a photo instead of a video clip? The template works best with a video clip. A photo may not give you the slow-motion and transition effects that make this edit look good.
