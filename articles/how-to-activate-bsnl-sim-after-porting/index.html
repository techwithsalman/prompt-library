How to Activate BSNL SIM After Porting: A Step-by-Step Guide

By Prompt Library • 28 July 2024

Are you thinking about switching to BSNL for better service and cheaper plans? You’re not alone! Many people are moving to BSNL these days.

This guide will walk you through the process of porting your number to BSNL and activating your new SIM card. Let’s get started!

Why Choose BSNL?

Before we dive into the activation process of BSNL sim, let’s quickly look at why BSNL is becoming a popular choice:

  • Cheaper tariff plans
  • Wide network coverage, especially in rural areas
  • Government-owned, providing stability
  • Improving service quality

How To Port BSNL Full Process

Porting to BSNL is a bit different from other companies. Here’s what you should keep in mind:

  • It takes longer (usually 7 days)
  • You need to plan ahead
  • The process is slightly more complex

But don’t worry! We’ll guide you through each step.

Step 1: Initiate the Porting Process

To start porting your number to BSNL, follow these steps:

Open your phone’s messaging app

Create a new message

In the ‘To’ field, type 1900

In the message body, type: PORT Your 10-digit mobile number

Send the message

Important: Make sure your current plan allows SMS services, or this won’t work!

Step 2: Get Your Unique Porting Code (UPC)

After sending the SMS, you’ll quickly receive a message with your UPC. It will look something like this:

“The UPC for your mobile number is JRT64239″

Remember:

  • The UPC is 8 digits long
  • It’s valid for only 5 days
  • Each SIM gets a different code

Step 3: Visit a BSNL Retailer or Office

Now that you have your UPC, it’s time to visit a BSNL retailer or office. Here’s what you need to do:

  1. Tell them you want to port your number to BSNL
  2. Provide your UPC
  3. Choose a new BSNL plan
  4. Fill out the necessary forms
  5. Provide required documents (usually ID and address proof)

Pro tip: Do this about 6 days before your current plan expires to avoid service interruption.

Step 4: Wait for the Porting Process

After submitting your request, you’ll get a confirmation message:

“Port request is received for your mobile number from BSNL. Porting will be completed on [date] at [time].”

Step 5: Activate Your New BSNL SIM

Once the porting is complete, it’s time to activate your new BSNL SIM. Here’s how:

  1. Turn off your phone
  2. Remove your old SIM
  3. Insert the new BSNL SIM
  4. Turn on your phone
  5. Wait for the network to connect (this may take a few minutes)

If your phone doesn’t connect automatically, try these steps:

  • Go to Settings > Network & Internet > Mobile Network
  • Select BSNL as your network operator

Step 6: Verify Your BSNL Connection

To make sure your BSNL SIM is working correctly:

  1. Make a test call
  2. Send a test SMS
  3. Try using mobile data

If any of these don’t work, contact BSNL customer care at 1800-180-1503.

Common Issues and Solutions

Sometimes, you might face some problems. Here are some common issues and how to fix them:

  1. No network after porting:
  • Wait for 24 hours
  • If still no network, contact BSNL customer care
  1. Unable to make calls:
  • Check if you have sufficient balance
  • Ensure you’ve completed the activation process
  1. No internet access:
  • Make sure mobile data is turned on
  • Check your APN settings (you can get these from BSNL’s website)

After Porting: Making the Most of Your BSNL Connection

Now that you’ve successfully ported to BSNL, here are some tips to get the best experience:

  • Explore BSNL apps: Download the MyBSNL app for easy recharges and plan management
  • Check for offers: BSNL often has special offers for new customers
  • Set up voicemail: Contact customer care to activate this useful feature
  • Enable roaming: If you travel often, ask about BSNL’s roaming packages

FAQ

Q: How long does the entire porting process to BSNL take?
A: The entire porting process to BSNL typically takes about 7 days from start to finish.

Q: Do I need to visit a BSNL store to complete the porting process?
A: Yes, you need to visit a BSNL retailer or office to submit your porting request and necessary documents.

Q: Can I keep my old number when porting to BSNL?
A: Yes, the whole point of porting is to keep your existing number while switching to BSNL.

Q: What documents do I need to port my number to BSNL?
A: You generally need a valid ID proof and address proof to port your number to BSNL.

Q: Will my service be interrupted during the porting process?
A: Your service should continue uninterrupted on your old SIM until the porting process is complete.

Q: What should I do if my BSNL SIM doesn’t activate after porting?
A: If your BSNL SIM doesn’t activate within 24 hours after the specified porting time, contact BSNL customer care for assistance.

Conclusion

Porting your number to BSNL might seem complicated, but if you follow these steps, you’ll be up and running in no time. Remember, the process takes about 7 days, so plan accordingly.

If you face any issues, don’t hesitate to reach out to BSNL customer care or visit your nearest BSNL office.

We hope this guide helps you smoothly transition to BSNL. Enjoy your new connection and the benefits it brings!
