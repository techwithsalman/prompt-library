Sad Nightwalk AI Video Editing Prompt For Flow AI

AI video editing is becoming very popular on social media. New AI tools allow users to create cinematic and emotional videos without using a professional camera or advanced editing software. One of the latest styles people are trying is the Sad Nightwalk AI Video.

In this style, a person is shown walking alone at night in a dark and cinematic environment. The video has a sad, emotional, and peaceful feeling. Street lights, light rain, reflections on the road, slow camera movement, and cinematic lighting can make the video look like a scene from a movie.

With Google Flow AI, you can create this type of AI video by using a simple text prompt. You do not need professional video-editing skills. In this article, we will explain how to create a Sad Nightwalk AI video using Flow AI.

What Is a Sad Nightwalk AI Video?

A Sad Nightwalk AI video is a short cinematic video that focuses on a lonely person walking at night. The main idea is to create an emotional atmosphere rather than a normal walking video.

The scene can include an empty street, city lights, rainy weather, wet roads, shadows, and a person walking slowly. The background music can later be added during editing to make the video more emotional.

This style works especially well for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok-style videos, and other short-video platforms.

How To Create Sad Nightwalk AI Video With Flow AI

Creating this video is quite simple if you have access to Flow AI. Follow the steps below.

Step 1: Prepare Your Photo

First, choose a clear photo that you want to use as the main reference for your AI video.

For the best result, use a photo where the person’s face is clearly visible. A full-body or medium shot can also work well because the AI needs enough information to understand the person’s appearance and clothing.

Try to use a high-quality image with good lighting. Avoid extremely blurry or heavily edited photos.

Step 2: Open Flow AI

Open Google Flow AI and start a new video generation project.

Depending on the options available in your Flow interface, upload your reference image and select the image-to-video workflow. The exact buttons or layout may change as the tool is updated.

The important part is to provide your photo as the visual reference and then describe the scene you want to create.

Step 3: Add the Sad Nightwalk Prompt

Now copy the following prompt and paste it into Flow AI.

Sad Nightwalk AI Video Prompt:

Create a cinematic and emotional nightwalk video using the uploaded image as the main character reference. Keep the person’s facial identity, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions, and overall appearance consistent with the reference image. Show the person walking alone on a quiet city street at night with a sad and thoughtful mood. The street is slightly wet after rain, creating beautiful reflections from street lights and distant city lights. Use soft blue and warm yellow cinematic lighting, realistic shadows, subtle fog, and a calm nighttime atmosphere. The person walks slowly and naturally while looking slightly down, as if lost in deep thoughts. Use a smooth cinematic camera movement that slowly follows the person from behind and slightly from the side. Add realistic walking motion, natural body movement, realistic clothing movement, and subtle wind. The environment should feel like a real movie scene, with shallow depth of field, realistic details, soft highlights, natural motion blur, and cinematic color grading. Make the video emotional, lonely, peaceful, and visually beautiful. Ultra-realistic cinematic photography, realistic human movement, detailed nighttime environment, high-quality film look, 9:16 vertical composition.

This prompt tells Flow AI about the character, location, lighting, camera movement, mood, and overall visual style.

Step 4: Generate the Video

After adding the prompt, generate the video.

AI video generation may take some time. The result can be different each time because the AI creates the motion based on your image and prompt.

If the first result does not look right, you can generate another version. You can also make small changes to the prompt.

For example, if you want more rain, add:

Add gentle rainfall throughout the scene with realistic water drops and reflections on the wet road.

If you want a more cinematic camera movement, add:

Use a slow tracking shot following the character while maintaining a smooth, stable camera movement.

Step 5: Make the Video More Emotional

The visual is only one part of the final video. You can make the result more emotional by editing it after generating the AI video.

You can use a mobile editing application to add slow emotional background music, a small amount of film grain, and subtle color adjustments.

Do not add too many effects. The Sad Nightwalk style usually looks better when the video remains simple and cinematic.

You can also slightly reduce the brightness and use cool tones to create a nighttime feeling.

Step 6: Use 9:16 Format

If your main goal is Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts, create the video in a 9:16 vertical format.

Vertical videos fill the entire smartphone screen and are better suited for short-form video platforms.

When generating the video, mention 9:16 vertical composition in your prompt. If the generated video does not have the correct framing, you can crop it later using your preferred video editor.

Tips For Better Results

Here are some simple tips that can help you create a better Sad Nightwalk AI video:

Use a clear reference photo: A sharp and clear image usually gives the AI more information about the character.

Keep the prompt detailed: Mention the environment, camera movement, lighting, mood, and motion you want.

Use natural movements: Avoid asking for complicated actions. Simple walking movements are usually easier for AI video models to generate naturally.

Try multiple generations: If one result has strange movements or an unnatural face, generate another version.

Keep the camera movement slow: Slow tracking shots work very well with emotional cinematic scenes.

Add music later: You can choose music that matches the mood of the video during the final editing process.

Final Thoughts

The Sad Nightwalk AI Video is a simple but powerful cinematic video concept. With Flow AI and a good reference image, you can turn an ordinary photo into a short nighttime movie scene.

The key to getting a good result is to use a clear image and a detailed prompt. Describe the character, environment, lighting, camera movement, and mood clearly. You can then generate multiple versions and choose the one that looks the most natural.

Once your AI video is ready, add suitable background music and make a few simple adjustments in your favorite video editor. Your final video can then be exported in 9:16 format and shared as an Instagram Reel, YouTube Short, or other vertical social-media video.

AI video tools are making cinematic content much easier for beginners. You do not need an expensive camera or advanced editing experience to experiment with creative video ideas. A good reference photo, a detailed prompt, and a little creativity can be enough to create an impressive Sad Nightwalk AI video.
