Thalapati Vijay Trending Helicopter AI Video Editing Prompt

If you want to create a similar Thalapati Vijay Trending Helicopter AI Video, you can now use a detailed multi-scene prompt with an AI video generation tool. The prompt in this article is designed around 17 connected scenes so the video feels like one continuous cinematic sequence instead of a collection of unrelated clips.

The most important part is your reference photo. Use a clear image of the person you want to feature in the video. The AI should use that image as the main identity reference throughout the scenes.

What Is the Thalapati Vijay Trending Helicopter AI Video?

The trend is based on a cinematic action sequence where a man arrives from a helicopter and walks through a dramatic red-earth landscape. The sequence gradually becomes more intense, with a gate, jeep, dust explosion, and motorcycle convoy appearing in different scenes.

The video is designed in a vertical 9:16 format, making it suitable for platforms such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and other short-video platforms.

Instead of creating just one simple clip, the prompt contains 17 scenes in a specific order. Each scene has its own camera angle and action while maintaining the same character, location, outfit progression, and cinematic color grading.

What You Need Before Starting

Before generating the video, prepare a good-quality reference photograph.

For the best result, choose a photo where the person’s:

  • Face is clearly visible
  • Hairstyle can be seen properly
  • Body proportions are natural
  • Clothing is reasonably clear
  • Image is not heavily blurred
  • Face is not covered by another object

The reference photo is important because the prompt asks the AI to maintain the same identity throughout the sequence.

Step 1: Upload Your Reference Photo

Open the AI video generation platform you want to use and look for its image-to-video or reference-image option.

Upload your selected photograph.

The uploaded image should be treated as the master reference for the character. The prompt specifically asks the AI to maintain the person’s face, hairstyle, skin tone, body build, height, and overall proportions.

This helps reduce unwanted changes between scenes.

Step 2: Copy the Full AI Video Prompt

After uploading the reference image, copy the complete 17-scene prompt from this article.

Do not remove the important instructions about identity preservation, location consistency, clothing, lighting, camera movement, or aspect ratio.

The prompt is intentionally detailed because every scene needs to connect with the next one.

The video should not suddenly change the person’s appearance or move to a completely different environment.

Step 3: Generate the Helicopter Opening

The first few scenes establish the cinematic introduction.

Scene 1 starts with an extreme close-up of the man’s leg and black sneaker as he steps out of the helicopter. The camera then reveals more of the character as he looks outward.

The following scenes move between close-ups and wider shots. The helicopter remains an important part of the environment while the character begins moving across the red quarry terrain.

This opening section should feel slow and powerful rather than rushed.

Step 4: Continue With the Walking Sequence

Scenes 5 through 7 focus more heavily on the character.

The man wears a black jacket or top with navy jogger-style pants, black sneakers, sunglasses, and a wristwatch. The camera moves between close-up portraits and full-body shots.

One scene shows him touching his collar or beard, while another focuses on him adjusting his collar with both hands.

These small movements are useful because they make the character feel like part of a movie sequence rather than simply standing in front of the camera.

Step 5: Create the Gate Sequence

The gate becomes the main visual element in Scenes 8 to 10.

First, the camera focuses on the old padlock and chain. The locked gate is then shown with the lock removed and lying on the ground.

Next, the character reaches toward the gate and pushes it open. His wristwatch should remain clearly visible.

The worm’s-eye-view shot in Scene 10 gives the sequence a dramatic transition. The camera looks upward from ground level as the gate opens and the character appears behind it.

Step 6: Add the Cinematic Portraits

Scene 11 returns to a close-up of the character.

The sunglasses and facial expression should remain consistent with the reference image. The camera starts extremely close and then pulls back slightly into a tighter portrait.

Warm sunlight and the red surroundings should continue the same visual style established earlier.

Scene 12 then changes to a wide shot. The character stands among small eroded rock and dirt formations. He first spreads his arms and later moves into a confident hands-on-hips pose.

The large pale sky creates plenty of visual space around the subject.

Step 7: Add the Jeep and Dust Explosion

Scene 13 introduces a parked jeep. Its side mirror and antenna appear slightly out of focus in the foreground while the character remains the main subject.

Then comes one of the biggest moments of the video.

In Scene 14, the character runs toward the camera while a large dust explosion develops behind him. At this point, his jacket has already been removed. He holds it in one hand while wearing a black T-shirt.

The dust should blend naturally with the red-earth environment rather than looking like an unrelated effect.

Step 8: Finish With the Motorcycle Convoy

Scene 15 shows the character throwing the jacket toward the camera. The jacket should have natural motion blur while the character maintains a confident expression.

Scene 16 becomes much wider and more energetic. A convoy of black motorcycles moves through the dusty red terrain, with riders performing wheelies in the foreground. The character runs through the center of the convoy.

The dark circular vignette helps give this scene an action-movie appearance.

Finally, Scene 17 returns to a close-up walking shot. The character walks through the motorcycle convoy with a calm swagger while the motorcycles and riders remain softly blurred behind him.

This gives the video a strong cinematic ending.

Keep the Location Consistent

One of the most important instructions in the prompt is location consistency.

The entire sequence should remain in the same red-earth quarry environment. The background should contain rust-colored rock formations, dusty terrain, green hills, and a pale sky.

The helicopter, gate, jeep, and motorcycles should feel like they belong to the same world.

If the AI suddenly changes the environment between scenes, regenerate that particular scene with stronger instructions about location continuity.

Maintain the Same Color Grade

The prompt uses a warm cinematic action-movie color style.

The main visual characteristics are:

  • Warm natural sunlight
  • Rich red and earthy brown tones
  • Strong contrast
  • Sharp details
  • Cinematic shadows
  • Natural skin tones
  • Dust in the atmosphere

Keeping the same color treatment throughout all 17 scenes makes the final video feel more professionally edited.

Add the Watermark

The prompt also requests a small semi-transparent “NoorEditing07” watermark in the bottom-right corner.

Keep it subtle so it does not cover the person’s face or important action.

If your AI video generator does not produce the watermark correctly, you can add it later using a normal video editing application.

How to Make the Final Video

If the AI tool generates each scene separately, download or save the generated clips and arrange them in the same order:

Scene 1 → Scene 2 → Scene 3 → … → Scene 17

Do not randomly change the sequence because the scenes are designed to build one continuous story.

You can then add suitable background music, sound effects, transitions, and small speed adjustments during final editing.

For a more cinematic result, avoid using too many flashy transitions. Simple cuts, smooth camera movement, dust effects, and well-timed sound effects usually work better for this type of action edit.

Final Tips for Better Results

A detailed prompt does not always produce a perfect result on the first attempt. AI video generation can sometimes change faces, clothing, hands, or background elements.

If one scene looks different, regenerate that scene instead of changing the entire project.

Also remember that identity consistency is especially important when creating a multi-scene video. Use the same reference image whenever the platform allows it.

For the best overall result, keep the same aspect ratio, character description, location description, lighting style, and color grade across every generation.

Final Words

The Thalapati Vijay Trending Helicopter AI Video Editing Prompt is designed for creators who want to turn a regular portrait into a dramatic action-style video. The 17-scene structure gives the sequence a clear beginning, middle, and ending.

From the helicopter entrance to the quarry walk, gate sequence, dust explosion, and motorcycle convoy, every scene adds another part to the cinematic story.

Simply upload your reference photo, paste the complete prompt, generate the scenes in order, and combine the clips during the final edit.

With consistent character identity, camera movement, lighting, and environment, you can create a much more convincing cinematic AI video suitable for sharing on short-video platforms.
