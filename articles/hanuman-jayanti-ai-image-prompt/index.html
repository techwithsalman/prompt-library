How To Create AI Photo With Hanumanji

So, let’s learn how to create this 3D photo with Hanuman Ji.

1. First, copy any of the prompt given above.

2. Then, go to the Bing Image Creator and paste the prompt.

3. To create the photo, you need to log in or sign up on the Bing Image Creator.

4. After logging in or signing up, paste the prompt and add your name. You can also change your age in the prompt.

5. Then, click on the “Create” button.

Within a short while, your photo with Hanuman Ji will be generated.

Finally, you can download the image and set it as your profile picture.
