Photo Prompt 👇

? AI PROMPT
Use the uploaded person photo as the STRICT face, identity, hairstyle and full-body reference. Create ONE premium ultra-realistic vertical 9:16 cinematic storyboard collage containing exactly 6 connected scenes arranged in a clean 2 columns × 3 rows layout. Generate one single collage image only. Keep all six panels equal in size with clean spacing. No panel numbers, no captions, no borders, no logo and no watermark. UNIVERSAL IDENTITY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY: This prompt must work naturally for any uploaded person — male, female, child, teenager, adult or elderly. Preserve the uploaded person’s exact real face, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, hairstyle, hairline, natural age, gender presentation, natural height, shoulder width, body shape, arm length, leg length and realistic body proportions. The same exact person must remain clearly recognizable in every panel. Never beautify, redesign or replace the face. Never make the subject taller, shorter, thinner, heavier, older, younger, more muscular, bigger or smaller. OUTFIT LOCK: Keep one identical outfit in all six panels: • oversized white half-sleeve graphic T-shirt • small scattered black abstract markings • one large metallic-gold abstract front motif • loose light-grey / faded ice-blue baggy jeans • clean white sneakers • simple wristwatch or bracelet Keep the exact same fitting, colours, fabric, sleeves, trousers and footwear throughout. LOCATION LOCK: Keep one identical rural field environment in every panel: • huge lush green paddy / rice fields • narrow walking path between tall crops • distant flat countryside horizon • tall electricity transmission towers • long overhead power lines • sparse distant trees • dramatic cloudy open sky • no city • no mountains • no background change COLOR GRADING LOCK: Use the same premium emotional golden-hour grading throughout: • warm golden sunlight • glowing sun behind thin clouds • slightly moody grey-blue clouds • rich natural green crops • natural skin tone • soft warm highlights • slightly cool shadows • gentle contrast • controlled saturation • soft atmospheric haze • subtle film grain • natural backlight rim around hair and shoulders SCENE 1 — GOLDEN PORTRAIT: Medium front three-quarter portrait of the person standing calmly beside the green field, looking slightly upward and to one side. Warm golden sunlight behind, face fully visible and sharp, power lines and distant transmission tower visible. SCENE 2 — SUNSET REAR SILHOUETTE: Rear medium-long shot of the same person facing the glowing sunset. Place the sun directly behind the head or shoulder, creating a soft golden halo. Calm posture, same outfit, same field. SCENE 3 — WIDE FIELD HERO SHOT: Very wide rear shot. The person stands on the narrow path in the middle of the paddy field and slowly opens both arms outward while facing the horizon. Huge cloudy sky and green fields dominate the composition. SCENE 4 — SIDE WALK: Medium side-profile full-body shot of the same person walking slowly along the narrow path beside tall rice plants. Head slightly lowered, calm expression, natural walking posture, same outfit and same field. SCENE 5 — FOOTSTEP POV: Downward cinematic view showing the same light-grey jeans and white sneakers walking naturally on the narrow grassy path between dense green rice plants. Show realistic feet, shoe contact and grass texture. SCENE 6 — FINAL ARMS-OPEN HERO: Front three-quarter medium-wide hero shot. The same person stands in the field, lifts the chin toward the glowing sky and opens both arms wide in a peaceful emotional pose. Keep the exact real face clearly visible and sharp. STYLE: Ultra-realistic live-action cinematography, authentic skin texture, realistic hair, correct anatomy, accurate hands and feet, natural clothing folds, detailed crops, cinematic depth of field, premium 4K/8K commercial quality. NEGATIVE PROMPT: No face change, no identity drift, no hairstyle change, no skin-tone change, no age change, no height change, no body change, no outfit change, no shoe change, no extra people, no duplicate subject, no extra limbs, no broken hands, no floating feet, no different field, no different pylons, no city, no mountains, no cartoon, no anime, no CGI skin, no text, no logo and no watermark.
