How to Use These Aesthetic Prompts for Boys

  1. Open Gemini, ChatGPT or any AI image tool you already use.
  2. Upload one clear, front-facing photo of yourself in good light — this decides how closely the face matches.
  3. Copy any prompt above and paste it together with your photo.
  4. If the face changes, add “keep face 100% same as uploaded photo” and generate again.

You can swap the outfit colour, the location or the lighting in any prompt to get a fresh result without rewriting the whole thing.
