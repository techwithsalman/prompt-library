The double exposure technique transforms a simple portrait into a storytelling masterpiece. It allows viewers to experience both the physical presence and emotional bond of the couple simultaneously.

Why Rainy Couple Portraits Are Trending

Romantic rain photography has become increasingly popular because it evokes genuine emotions. Unlike studio portraits, rainy outdoor photos feel spontaneous, natural, and cinematic.
