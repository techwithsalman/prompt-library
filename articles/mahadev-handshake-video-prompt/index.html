Handshake With Lord Mahadev Photo and Video Prompt

By Prompt Library • 25 June 2026

Have you seen that viral video where a boy is standing in front of a huge Lord Shiva mural on a wall — and the moment he extends his hand, Shiva’s hand slowly comes out of the wall to hold it?

It looks absolutely magical. And the best part? You can make one with your own photo. No special skills needed.

I tried this myself, and honestly, the result was stunning. In this guide, I’ll show you exactly how to do it — step by step — using just ChatGPT and Google Flow AI.

What Is the Lord Shiva Handshake Trend?

This is one of the most emotional and spiritual AI video trends on Instagram right now.

Here’s what happens in the video:

  • A person stands in a dark Indian alley, looking at a large Shiva mural painted on an old wall
  • They slowly raise their hand toward the mural
  • Shiva’s hand appears to come out of the wall in 3D
  • The two hands meet — it feels divine and cinematic

People are loving this because it connects faith with technology. Even though everyone knows it’s made with AI, they still feel something watching it. That’s the power of a good idea.

What You Need Before Starting

  • A full body photo of yourself (your whole body should be visible — not just face)
  • A ChatGPT account (free works, but GPT-4o gives better image quality)
  • A Google Flow AI account (go to labs.google/flow)
  • A good internet connection

That’s it. No app downloads. No complicated tools.

Step 1 — Create the AI Photo Using ChatGPT

First, we create the base image. This photo will show you standing in front of a Lord Shiva mural in a cinematic night setting.

How to do it:

  1. Open ChatGPT
  2. Click the “+” icon to attach your photo
  3. Upload a full body photo of yourself
  4. Paste the photo prompt below in the message box
  5. Send it and wait for ChatGPT to generate the image
  6. Download and save the image to your phone or computer

Important: Use a photo where your full body is clearly visible. The better your original photo, the better the final result.

Once ChatGPT gives you the image, save it. Now we move to the next step.

Step 2 — Turn the Photo Into a Video Using Google Flow AI

Now comes the magic. We take that photo and animate it — adding the Shiva handshake effect using Google Flow AI.

How to do it:

  1. Go to Google Flow AI
  2. Click “Create Project”
  3. Click the “+” icon to upload your ChatGPT image
  4. In the video prompt box at the bottom, paste the video prompt below
  5. Click Generate and wait (it takes a few minutes)
  6. Once ready, preview the video and hit Save to download it in high quality

Tips for a Better Result

  • Full body photo is a must. If only your face or half body is visible, the AI won’t place you properly in the scene.
  • Use a plain background photo if possible. It helps ChatGPT focus on you.
  • If the first result doesn’t look right, try again — sometimes you need 2–3 attempts to get a great image.
  • Google Flow AI may take a few minutes to generate the video. Don’t close the tab while it’s processing.
  • After downloading, you can add a devotional song in the background using CapCut before posting on Instagram Reels.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Can I use a face-only photo instead of a full body photo? No. For this video to look good, your whole body must be visible in the original photo. A face-only photo won’t work well with this prompt.

Q2. Is ChatGPT free to use for image generation? ChatGPT’s free plan has limited image generation. For best results, GPT-4o (available on ChatGPT Plus) gives sharper, more realistic images.

Q3. Is Google Flow AI free? Yes, Google Flow AI is currently free to use. You just need a Google account to sign in at labs.google/flow.

Q4. My video doesn’t look realistic. What went wrong? Two common reasons: (1) Your original photo wasn’t a clear full-body shot, or (2) the photo prompt wasn’t followed exactly. Try again with a better photo and copy the prompts carefully.

Q5. Can I make this video for someone else — like my parents or friends? Absolutely! Just use their full-body photo and follow the same steps. It makes a very meaningful and touching gift.

Q6. Can I post this on Instagram Reels? Yes! This type of video does very well on Instagram Reels. Add a Mahadev bhajan or devotional background music using CapCut before posting for best engagement.

Q7. How long does Google Flow AI take to generate the video? Usually 2–5 minutes. Just keep the tab open and don’t refresh the page while it’s generating.
