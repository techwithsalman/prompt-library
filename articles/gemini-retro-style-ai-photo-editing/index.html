How to Create Retro Vintage-Style Photos with Gemini AI

Follow these simple steps to create your own trending retro-style photo:

  1. Download Google Gemini App → Install it from Play Store or App Store.
  2. Sign In → Use your Google account to access Gemini AI.
  3. Upload a Photo → Add your own picture (or your crush’s if you’re a boy 😉).
  4. Paste a Prompt → Copy one of the above Gemini prompts.
  5. Generate the Image → Click the “Generate” button and wait a few seconds.
  6. Download & Share → Save the photo and share it on Instagram, Facebook, or anywhere else to get likes and comments.
