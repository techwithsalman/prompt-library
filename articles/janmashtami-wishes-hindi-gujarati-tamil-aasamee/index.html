Best Janmashtami Wishes in Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Assamese

By Prompt Library • 26 August 2024

Janmashtami, the joyous festival celebrating the birth of Lord Krishna, is just around the corner. As we welcome the divine child on August 19, 2024, let’s explore some heartfelt Janmashtami wishes in different languages to share with our loved ones.

Whether you prefer to express your devotion in Tamil, Hindi, Gujarati, or Assamese, we’ve got you covered with various messages.

Happy Janmashtami Wishes in Tamil

Tamil Nadu has a rich tradition of celebrating Janmashtami, known locally as Gokulashtami. Here are some warm wishes in Tamil to share with your family and friends:

“கிருஷ்ண ஜெயந்தி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்! உங்கள் வாழ்க்கையில் கண்ணனின் அருள் என்றும் நிறைந்திருக்கட்டும்.”
(Krishna Jayanthi Nalvazhthukkal! Ungal vaazhkayil Kannanin arul endrum niraindhirukkattum.)
Meaning: Happy Krishna Jayanthi! May Krishna’s blessings always fill your life.

“கோகுலாஷ்டமி வாழ்த்துக்கள்! கண்ணன் உங்கள் வீட்டில் மகிழ்ச்சியையும் செழிப்பையும் கொண்டு வரட்டும்.”
(Gokulashtami vaazhthukkal! Kannan ungal veettil magizhchiyaiyum sezhippaiyum kondu varattum.)
Meaning: Gokulashtami greetings! May Krishna bring happiness and prosperity to your home.

“ஜென்மாஷ்டமி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்! கிருஷ்ணரின் அன்பும் கருணையும் உங்களை வழிநடத்தட்டும்.”
(Janmashtami nalvazhthukkal! Krishnarin anbum karunaiyum ungalai vazhinadathattum.)
Meaning: Happy Janmashtami! May Krishna’s love and compassion guide you.

“கண்ணன் பிறந்த நாள் வாழ்த்துக்கள்! உங்கள் வாழ்க்கை கிருஷ்ணரின் லீலைகளைப் போல் இனிமையாக இருக்கட்டும்.”
(Kannan pirantha naal vaazhthukkal! Ungal vaazhkai Krishnarin leelaikalaim pol inimaiyaaga irukkattum.)
Meaning: Happy Krishna’s birthday! May your life be as sweet as Krishna’s divine play.

“ஜெய் கோவிந்தா! இந்த ஜென்மாஷ்டமி உங்கள் வாழ்வில் புதிய நம்பிக்கையையும் மகிழ்ச்சியையும் கொண்டு வரட்டும்.”
(Jai Govinda! Indha Janmashtami ungal vaazhvil pudhiya nambikkaiyaiyum magizhchiyaiyum kondu varattum.)
Meaning: Jai Govinda! May this Janmashtami bring new hope and joy to your life.

“கிருஷ்ண ஜெயந்தி வாழ்த்துக்கள்! கண்ணனின் குழலோசை உங்கள் வாழ்வில் இன்னிசையாக ஒலிக்கட்டும்.”
(Krishna Jayanthi vaazhthukkal! Kannanin kuzholosai ungal vaazhvil innisaiyaaga olikattum.)
Meaning: Happy Krishna Jayanthi! May the melody of Krishna’s flute resound as sweet music in your life.

“ஜென்மாஷ்டமி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்! கிருஷ்ணரின் தூய அன்பு உங்கள் இதயத்தை நிரப்பட்டும்.”
(Janmashtami nalvazhthukkal! Krishnarin thooya anbu ungal idhayathai nirappattum.)
Meaning: Happy Janmashtami! May Krishna’s pure love fill your heart.

Janmashtami Wishes Images in Hindi

For those who prefer to share their Janmashtami wishes visually, here are some ideas for creating beautiful images with Hindi text. You can use these messages to create eye-catching graphics:

“श्री कृष्ण जन्माष्टमी की हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ!”
(Shri Krishna Janmashtami ki hardik shubhkamnayein!)
Meaning: Heartfelt wishes for Sri Krishna Janmashtami!

“कान्हा की कृपा सदा आप पर बनी रहे। जन्माष्टमी की बधाई!”
(Kanha ki kripa sada aap par bani rahe. Janmashtami ki badhai!)
Meaning: May Kanha’s blessings always be upon you. Congratulations on Janmashtami!

“मुरली मनोहर श्री कृष्ण की जयंती पर आपको और आपके परिवार को हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ!”
(Murli Manohar Shri Krishna ki jayanti par aapko aur aapke parivar ko hardik shubhkamnayein!)
Meaning: Heartfelt wishes to you and your family on the birth anniversary of Murli Manohar Shri Krishna!

“गोकुल में बाजे बधाई, मथुरा में बजी बधाई, आज प्रभु श्री कृष्ण का जन्मदिन आई!”
(Gokul mein baaje badhai, Mathura mein baji badhai, aaj prabhu Shri Krishna ka janamdin aai!)
Meaning: Celebrations in Gokul, celebrations in Mathura, today is Lord Sri Krishna’s birthday!

“माखन चोर नंद किशोर की जयंती पर आपको ढेर सारी शुभकामनाएँ!”
(Makhan chor Nand Kishore ki jayanti par aapko dher saari shubhkamnayein!)
Meaning: Lots of good wishes to you on the birth anniversary of the butter thief, Nand Kishore!

“कृष्ण के प्रेम और भक्ति से भरा हो आपका जीवन। जन्माष्टमी की हार्दिक बधाई!”
(Krishna ke prem aur bhakti se bhara ho aapka jeevan. Janmashtami ki hardik badhai!)
Meaning: May your life be filled with Krishna’s love and devotion. Heartfelt congratulations on Janmashtami!

“गोपाल की लीला अपरंपार, जन्माष्टमी का त्योहार मुबारक हो आपको हजार बार!”
(Gopal ki leela aprampar, Janmashtami ka tyohar mubarak ho aapko hazaar baar!)
Meaning: Gopal’s divine play is infinite, may the festival of Janmashtami be blessed to you a thousand times!

Janmashtami Wishes in Gujarati

Gujarat has a special connection with Lord Krishna, as it’s believed he ruled the kingdom of Dwarka. Here are some heartfelt Janmashtami wishes in Gujarati:

“શ્રી કૃષ્ણ જન્માષ્ટમીના શુભ અવસર પર આપને અને આપના પરિવારને ખૂબ ખૂબ શુભેચ્છાઓ!”
(Shri Krishna Janmashtamina shubh avasar par aapne ane aapna parivarne khub khub shubhechchhao!)
Meaning: Many good wishes to you and your family on the auspicious occasion of Shri Krishna Janmashtami!

“કાન્હાની કૃપા સદાય આપના પર વરસતી રહે. જન્માષ્ટમીની હાર્દિક શુભકામનાઓ!”
(Kaanhani krupa sadaay aapna par varasati rahe. Janmashtamini hardik shubhkamnao!)
Meaning: May Kanha’s blessings always shower upon you. Heartfelt good wishes for Janmashtami!

“શ્રી કૃષ્ણની જન્મજયંતી પર આપને અને આપના પરિવારને અઢળક શુભેચ્છાઓ!”
(Shri Krishnani janmajayanti par aapne ane aapna parivarne adhalak shubhechchhao!)
Meaning: Abundant good wishes to you and your family on Shri Krishna’s birth anniversary!

“મોરપીંછધારી મુરલીધરની કૃપા સદાય આપ પર બની રહે. જન્માષ્ટમીની શુભેચ્છાઓ!”
(Morpinchhadhari Murlidharni krupa sadaay aap par bani rahe. Janmashtamini shubhechchhao!)
Meaning: May the blessings of the peacock feather-wearing flute player always be with you. Good wishes for Janmashtami!

“આ જન્માષ્ટમી આપના જીવનમાં સુખ, સમૃદ્ધિ અને આનંદ લાવે એવી શુભેચ્છા!”
(Aa Janmashtami aapna jeevanma sukh, samruddhi ane anand laave evi shubhechchha!)
Meaning: Wishing that this Janmashtami brings happiness, prosperity, and joy to your life!

“શ્રી કૃષ્ણની ભક્તિ અને પ્રેમથી આપનું જીવન ભરપૂર રહે. જન્માષ્ટમીની હાર્દિક શુભકામનાઓ!”
(Shri Krishnani bhakti ane premthi aapnu jeevan bharpoor rahe. Janmashtamini hardik shubhkamnao!)
Meaning: May your life be full of devotion and love for Shri Krishna. Heartfelt good wishes for Janmashtami!

“ગોકુળમાં વાગી વધાઈ, મથુરામાં વાગી વધાઈ, આજે પ્રભુ શ્રી કૃષ્ણનો જન્મદિવસ આવ્યો!”
(Gokulma vaagi vadhai, Mathurama vaagi vadhai, aaje prabhu Shri Krishnano janmdivas aavyo!)
Meaning: Celebrations in Gokul, celebrations in Mathura, today is Lord Shri Krishna’s birthday!

Shubh Janmashtami Wishes in Hindi

Hindi is widely spoken across India, making it a popular choice for Janmashtami wishes. Here are some auspicious greetings in Hindi:

“श्री कृष्ण जन्माष्टमी की शुभकामनाएँ! आपका जीवन कृष्ण की भक्ति से भरा रहे।”
(Shri Krishna Janmashtami ki shubhkamnayein! Aapka jeevan Krishna ki bhakti se bhara rahe.)
Meaning: Best wishes for Shri Krishna Janmashtami! May your life be filled with devotion to Krishna.

“जन्माष्टमी के इस पावन अवसर पर आपको और आपके परिवार को हार्दिक शुभकामनाएँ!”
(Janmashtami ke is paavan avasar par aapko aur aapke parivar ko hardik shubhkamnayein!)
Meaning: Heartfelt good wishes to you and your family on this sacred occasion of Janmashtami!

“कृष्ण की मुरली की मधुर धुन आपके जीवन में सुख और शांति लाए। शुभ जन्माष्टमी!”
(Krishna ki murli ki madhur dhun aapke jeevan mein sukh aur shanti laye. Shubh Janmashtami!)
Meaning: May the sweet melody of Krishna’s flute bring happiness and peace to your life. Happy Janmashtami!

“गोकुल का लाला, मथुरा का राजा, द्वारका का नाथ आपकी हर मनोकामना पूरी करे। जन्माष्टमी की हार्दिक बधाई!”
(Gokul ka lala, Mathura ka raja, Dwarka ka nath aapki har manokamna poori kare. Janmashtami ki hardik badhai!)
Meaning: May the darling of Gokul, the king of Mathura, the lord of Dwarka fulfill all your wishes. Heartfelt congratulations on Janmashtami!

“माखन चोर, नटखट नंद किशोर की कृपा सदा आप पर बनी रहे। शुभ जन्माष्टमी!”
(Makhan chor, natkhat Nand Kishore ki kripa sada aap par bani rahe. Shubh Janmashtami!)
Meaning: May the blessings of the butter thief, the mischievous Nand Kishore, always be upon you. Happy Janmashtami!

“कन्हैया की मुरली बजे, राधा का नाच हो, गोपियों का रास हो, ऐसी जन्माष्टमी का जश्न मनाएँ!”
(Kanhaiya ki murli baje, Radha ka naach ho, gopiyon ka raas ho, aisi Janmashtami ka jashn manayein!)
Meaning: Let Kanhaiya’s flute play, let Radha dance, let the gopis perform raas, let’s celebrate such a Janmashtami!
