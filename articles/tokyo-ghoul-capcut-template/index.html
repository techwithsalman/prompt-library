What Is the Tokyo Ghoul CapCut Template?

Tokyo Ghoul is a famous Japanese anime known for its dark story, red and black color style, and intense atmosphere.

The Tokyo Ghoul CapCut template brings that same feel to your videos. It is a ready-made editing template inside the CapCut app. You just add your photos or video clips — and the template turns them into a cinematic, anime-style edit automatically.

No manual editing. No effects to set. No experience needed.

Why Is This Template So Popular Right Now?

I have personally used dozens of CapCut templates. Here is why the Tokyo Ghoul one stands out:

It Gives a Dark, Cinematic Look

The template uses deep dark tones, red highlights, and moody lighting — all inspired by the anime. It makes even a simple selfie look like a movie scene.

The Transitions Are Already Set

You do not have to figure out when a transition happens. Everything is pre-timed. One clip flows into the next smoothly and perfectly.

The Music Is Already Trending

The audio used in this template is already popular on social media. And trending audio means more reach — Instagram and TikTok both push videos that use trending sounds.

Anyone Can Use It

If you have never edited a video before, you can still make this. It is that simple.

How to Use the Tokyo Ghoul CapCut Template – Step by Step

This whole process takes about 5 minutes.

Step 1 – Click the Template Link

Click the link below. It will open the Tokyo Ghoul template directly inside the CapCut app on your phone.

Use On CapCut

Can’t open the link? Open CapCut app → tap the Templates tab at the bottom → search for “Tokyo Ghoul”

Step 2 – Tap “Use Template”

Once the template opens, you will see a button that says “Use Template”. Tap it.

Step 3 – Add Your Photos or Videos

The template will show you how many clips it needs — for example, 5 photos or 3 video clips. Just pick them from your phone gallery.

Quick tip: Close-up face photos and full-body shots work best with this template. Dark or moody photos look even more dramatic.

Step 4 – Preview and Export

After you add your clips, CapCut will build the edit automatically. Watch the preview once. If you like it, tap the Export button. The video saves to your phone.

Step 5 – Post It on Instagram or TikTok

Your video is ready. Upload it to Instagram Reels or TikTok. Use trending hashtags like:

#TokyoGhoul #CapCutTemplate #AnimeEdit #ViralReel #CapCut2026

What Kind of Photos Work Best?

Based on my own experience using this template, here is what looks great:

  • Dark background photos — the template’s effects pop even more
  • Close-up selfies — your face stands out with the dramatic filter
  • Attitude or serious poses — matches the anime vibe perfectly
  • Night photos or low-light shots — gives a natural cinematic feel

Avoid: Very bright, colorful, or outdoor daylight photos. They clash with the dark theme of this template.

Tips to Actually Go Viral With This Template

Using the template is just step one. Here is what actually helps you get more views:

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the Tokyo Ghoul CapCut template free? Yes, it is completely free. You just need the CapCut app installed on your phone.

Does this template work on iPhone and Android? Yes. CapCut is available on both iOS and Android. The template works on both.

How many photos do I need for this template? It depends on the version, but most Tokyo Ghoul templates need around 5 to 7 photos or short video clips.

Can I use this template without knowing anime? Absolutely. You do not have to be an anime fan to use this template. Anyone can use it and the edit will still look amazing.

My link is not opening in CapCut. What should I do? Make sure the CapCut app is installed and updated. If the link still does not work, open CapCut manually, go to the Templates tab, and search “Tokyo Ghoul” to find it.

Will this template help me get more followers? Using trending templates definitely increases your chances of going viral, which can bring new followers. But consistency matters more — keep posting regularly.
