How to Use ChatGPT Photo Editing Prompts

ChatGPT

So I’ve been playing around with ChatGPT to make some really cool photos, and honestly, it’s way easier than I thought it would be. You just need to know how to ask for what you want properly.

The trick is being really specific about what you want. Like instead of saying “make me a photo of a cat,” you’d say something like “create a realistic photo of a fluffy orange cat sitting on a wooden table with soft natural lighting.” The more details you give it, the better your photo turns out.

I use this for everything now – making pictures for my Instagram posts, blog images, even some stuff for work presentations. It saves me so much time because I don’t have to spend hours looking for the perfect stock photo or hiring someone to take pictures for me.
