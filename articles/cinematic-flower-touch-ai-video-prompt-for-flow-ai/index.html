Create an exact 10-second, 16:9 landscape, 30fps, normal real-time-speed, ultra-photorealistic cinematic live-action video recreating ONLY the original physical performance and camera footage of the uploaded reference video; use the uploaded character reference as the strict identity source if provided, preserving the exact face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, facial hair, body build, height and natural proportions with zero identity drift. The scene takes place in a lush flower garden filled with dense green foliage and a large bougainvillea-like flowering bush covered with abundant bright pink flowers; warm natural late-afternoon sunlight illuminates the flowers and the woman, with soft green background bokeh, shallow depth of field, natural highlights, realistic skin tones, rich pink-and-green colors and a soft cinematic photographic look. Preserve the exact visible outfit: a light/white long-sleeve top with an all-over blue floral print, fitted natural silhouette, and the same hairstyle: dark hair gathered into a low neat bun secured with a small blue flower hair clip, with a few loose strands around the face; no outfit or hairstyle changes. SCENE 1 — 0.0–2.2s: begin with a very close shallow-depth-of-field shot of a pink flowering branch and green leaves in the foreground; the woman's hand enters from the lower/right area and gently reaches toward the flowers, lightly touching/holding a small branch or flower cluster with relaxed fingers; part of her blue-floral sleeve and shoulder is visible at the edge of frame; camera remains close to the flowers with natural subtle movement and the background heavily blurred. SCENE 2 — 2.2–4.7s: hard cut to a medium rear shot of the woman standing directly in front of the dense flowering bush; her back faces the camera, shoulders relaxed, and her right hand is raised beside the right side of her head as she gently adjusts/touches her hair near the ear/temple; her head is slightly angled toward the flowers, with the blue flower hair clip clearly visible in the low bun; she remains mostly stationary while the camera holds a stable intimate composition and the pink flowers fill the entire background. SCENE 3 — 4.7–6.8s: hard cut to a closer rear three-quarter view; she slowly lowers her right hand away from her hair and subtly turns her head/body toward the flowering bush, looking at the flowers immediately in front of her; her loose hair strand moves naturally and the floral-print fabric remains clearly visible; camera makes only a gentle natural lateral/forward movement while maintaining the close framing. SCENE 4 — 6.8–8.0s: hard cut to a wider rear shot revealing more of the woman from approximately waist-up and a larger section of the flowering bush; she stands facing the flowers and makes a small natural step/weight shift closer to them, keeping her posture relaxed; her right arm begins moving forward toward a flower branch while her left arm remains relaxed; camera remains steady with subtle cinematic depth and foreground leaves softly framing the shot. SCENE 5 — 8.0–10.0s: hard cut to a close rear three-quarter shot focused on her upper back, bun and right hand; she extends her right hand toward a nearby pink flower cluster and gently holds/brushes the flowers with her fingertips, looking toward the flower she is touching; keep her head slightly turned toward the flowers, blue hair clip clearly visible, and preserve the delicate hand/finger movement exactly and naturally; the final frame holds on her hand near the pink flowers with dense green foliage and soft pink bokeh behind. Maintain the exact chronological performance: flower close-up with hand touching flowers → rear shot adjusting hair → rear three-quarter head/body turn toward flowers → wider approach/hand movement → close shot gently touching flower cluster. Preserve realistic finger anatomy, subtle wrist movement, natural head turns, body weight shifts, fabric folds, hair strands, flower/leaf movement and sunlight. Each shot is a distinct hard cut; do not merge the shots into one continuous camera take. Ignore any text, lyrics, captions, color-filter changes, artificial blur or other post-production effects from the reference; reproduce only the underlying live-action performance, natural camera behavior, lighting and environment. STRICT NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS: no identity drift, no face morphing, no hairstyle change, no outfit change, no extra people, no extra props, no extra flowers invented, no different location, no invented choreography, no dancing, no exaggerated gestures, no random camera movement, no excessive zoom, no slow motion, no speed ramp, no time remapping, no freeze frame, no artificial motion blur, no artificial transitions, no text, no subtitles, no captions, no lyrics, no logo, no watermark, no dialogue, no narration, no voice; reproduce the reference video's exact visual language, character positioning, hand movement, hair movement, flower interaction, camera framing, camera movement, lighting, depth of field and lush garden atmosphere as faithfully as possible, with exactly 10 seconds of normal real-time footage.
