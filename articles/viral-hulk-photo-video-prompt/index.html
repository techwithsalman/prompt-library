Viral Hulk Photo-Video Prompt & Free Hulk Reels Bundle

By Prompt Library • 30 December 2025

Have you ever scrolled through YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels and seen a massive, realistic Hulk living in a village, emotional and crying, or fighting to save his family? These videos are currently a goldmine for creators.

In this guide, I will break down the exact step-by-step process used by top creators to make viral Hulk AI videos using tools like ChatGPT and specialized AI video generators.

The Secret Formula: The 4 Pillars of a Viral AI Story

Before you generate a single image, you must understand why these videos go viral. A successful Hulk AI video isn’t just about the graphics; it’s about the emotional hook. Every viral script follows this 4-part structure:

  1. Shocking: Start with something unexpected (e.g., Hulk’s father is missing).
  2. Emotional: Show a vulnerable side of the character to connect with the audience.
  3. Anger: Create a conflict or a villain that makes the viewers root for Hulk.
  4. Happiness: End with a resolution or a “win” (e.g., Hulk winning a wrestling match to save his dad).

Step 1: Setting Up Your Character in ChatGPT

To keep your Hulk looking consistent throughout the video, you need to “train” ChatGPT on your character.

  1. Upload an image: Find or generate a high-quality 3D image of Hulk.
  2. Give the Instruction: Tell ChatGPT: “Analyze this image and consider this character as ‘Hulk’. From now on, whenever I give you a situation, generate a Video Prompt (VO Prompt) based on this character’s appearance.”
  3. The Dialogue Advantage: You can speak to ChatGPT in Hindi or English. Simply describe the scene (e.g., “Hulk is crying because he has no money”) and ask ChatGPT to convert it into a detailed visual AI prompt.

Step 2: Generating the Visuals (The “Brave” Method)

While there are many AI tools, the current trend involves using high-end video generators like Fleo (or similar platforms like Luma or Kling) via a secure browser like Brave.

Using Text-to-Video:

  1. Copy your prompt from ChatGPT.
  2. Open the Video AI Tool: Go to your video generation platform (e.g., Fleo).
  3. New Project: Select the “Text-to-Video” format.
  4. Paste & Generate: Paste your detailed prompt. Wait for the progress bar to hit 100%.

Pro Tip: If you don’t like the background or the lighting, don’t settle! Go back to ChatGPT, ask it to “change the background to a rainy village night,” and re-run the prompt.

Step 3: Viral Hulk Prompts to Get You Started

Act as a YouTube Shorts script writer.

Story requirements:
– Start the story with the PROBLEM in the FIRST LINE itself (no buildup)
– Every line must feel urgent, emotional, and fast-paced
– Characters should speak naturally like real village conversations
– Keep the tone dramatic, emotional, and heroic
– The story must feel like a viral YouTube Shorts script

I want a PURE DIALOGUE-BASED STORY SCRIPT in Hindi.
❌ Do NOT add narration, description, or scene explanation.
✅ Write ONLY character dialogues line by line.

Characters:
1. Hulk (main character, emotional + powerful)
2. Hulk ki maa
3. Randu Baba (villain)

Story flow (but ONLY through dialogues):
– Immediate problem at the start (shock line)
– Conflict & struggle through conversations
– Challenge given to Hulk
– Hulk takes a risky decision
– High-energy climax
– Clear Call To Action spoken by Hulk (like: “dosto mujhe aapki zarurat hai”)
– Strong resolution at the end

Extra rules:
– No headings
– No scene names
– No explanations
– Only spoken dialogues
– Short and punchy lines suitable for 60–90 seconds Shorts

Language: Hindi

If you’re looking for high-engagement ideas, try these specific prompts:

  • The Emotional Hook: “Hulk sitting on a wooden bench in a small village, head in hands, looking heartbroken, hyper-realistic skin textures, 8k cinematic lighting, emotional atmosphere.”
  • The Action Hook: “Hulk entering a dusty village wrestling ring, angry expression, muscles bulging, crowd cheering in the background, cinematic slow motion.”
  • The Victory Hook: “Hulk holding a trophy and a bag of money, smiling with tears in his eyes, soft sunset lighting, heartwarming 3D animation style.”

Step 4: Final Editing and Voiceover

To make your video “Social Media Ready,” follow these finishing touches:

  • Voiceover: Use ElevenLabs to create a deep, grittier voice for Hulk and a softer voice for the mother/villagers.
  • Captions: Use CapCut to add bold, colorful captions.
  • The Engagement Trick: In the middle of your video, ask your viewers to “Like and Subscribe to give Hulk strength.” This psychological trigger significantly boosts your subscriber count.
