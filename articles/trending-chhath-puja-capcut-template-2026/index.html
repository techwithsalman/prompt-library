Why Is Chhath Puja Celebrated?

Chhath Puja is an important Hindu festival dedicated to the worship of Surya Dev, the Sun God, and Chhathi Maiya. People observe this festival to express gratitude for the Sun, seek blessings for the well-being and prosperity of their families, and pray for a healthy and fulfilling life. The festival is known for its traditional rituals, fasting, prayers, and offerings made near rivers, ponds, and other water bodies. Chhath Puja is especially popular in Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, and other parts of India, as well as among communities living in different countries.
