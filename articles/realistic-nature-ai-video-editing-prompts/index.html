Use the uploaded clear person photo as the ONLY, PRIMARY and ABSOLUTE identity reference. Create ONE continuous premium ultra-realistic horizontal 16:9 cinematic nature video, approximately 10 seconds long, containing all connected scenes in one sequence. The final result must look like genuine professionally filmed real live-action footage, not AI-looking footage. ================================================== ABSOLUTE IDENTITY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY ================================================== Identity consistency is more important than motion, background or cinematic style. Preserve the uploaded person’s exact real: face shape, forehead, eyes, eye spacing, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, chin, ears, skin tone, natural skin texture, facial asymmetry, hairstyle, hairline, natural age, natural height, shoulder width, body shape, arm length, leg length, head-to-body ratio and realistic proportions. The person shown in every scene must remain the EXACT SAME real person from the uploaded photo. Never create a similar-looking replacement person. Never beautify, redesign, reconstruct or morph the face. Never change: eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, hairstyle, age, height, body size or proportions. Use one permanently locked face and one permanently locked body throughout the complete video. Whenever the face becomes visible after a back shot, restore the identity directly from the original uploaded photo. No face change. No identity drift. No face morphing. No face swap. No beauty filter. No body change. ================================================== OUTFIT LOCK ================================================== Keep one identical outfit throughout the entire video: • oversized clean white full-sleeve shirt • loose dark charcoal / black trousers • simple dark footwear Keep the exact same shirt shape, sleeve length, loose fit, trouser fit, colour and fabric in every scene. Do not change outfit in any shot. ================================================== LOCATION LOCK ================================================== Keep the same exact location throughout: • peaceful green park / garden • tall slim trees with light-brown trunks • fresh bright green leaves • soft green grass and decorative long plants • open natural walking area • calm daylight nature atmosphere • no crowd • no buildings • no city • no vehicles Every scene must feel filmed in the same natural tree garden. ================================================== COLOUR GRADING LOCK ================================================== Keep the same premium natural cinematic grading throughout: • warm-green golden daylight • rich fresh greens • soft natural highlights • natural skin tone • slightly soft dreamy contrast • subtle yellow-green sunlight feel • clean cinematic background blur • premium outdoor reel look • soft realistic shadows • gentle film-like depth Do not use fake HDR. Do not oversaturate. Do not create orange skin. Do not create artificial CGI lighting. ================================================== FULL VIDEO FLOW — EXACT SCENES ================================================== SCENE 1 — 0.0 to 1.5 seconds Begin with a rear medium-wide shot. The same person walks slowly and naturally forward through the peaceful green tree garden. The body is shown from the back / slight rear three-quarter angle. White shirt and dark trousers are clearly visible. Use a slow stabilized camera follow. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 2 — 1.5 to 3.0 seconds Continue the same walk. The person moves deeper into the garden between the trees. Use a rear three-quarter shot from slightly closer distance. Plants and tree trunks pass naturally in the background. Keep body movement calm and realistic. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 3 — 3.0 to 4.4 seconds The person gradually slows down and stops near a tree. Use a rear medium-long shot. The subject stands quietly and looks ahead into the green park. Subtle natural breathing only. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 4 — 4.4 to 5.8 seconds Use a soft transition to a slightly different angle in the same location. The person remains standing calmly near the tree. Use a slow side-rear cinematic move. Keep the same outfit, same body, same hairstyle and same location continuity. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 5 — 5.8 to 7.2 seconds Show a clean medium shot from the side / side-back angle. The person slightly turns the upper body and head in a calm reflective manner. Do not use a full dramatic turn. Movement must stay slow and natural. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 6 — 7.2 to 8.6 seconds Now reveal a clearer side / front-three-quarter face view. IMPORTANT: At this moment, restore the face directly from the uploaded original photo. Show the exact same real face with: same eyes, same eyebrows, same nose, same lips, same jawline, same hairstyle, same skin tone. The person looks softly downward or slightly to one side in a calm emotional mood. -------------------------------------------------- SCENE 7 — 8.6 to 10.0 seconds Final premium hero shot. Use a medium close side / front-three-quarter composition. The person stands peacefully beside the tree with the green background softly blurred. Only subtle blinking, breathing and a very small natural head movement. Keep the exact uploaded face visible and unchanged until the final frame. End naturally with the same peaceful park atmosphere. ================================================== FACE STABILITY RULES ================================================== Face accuracy is more important than dramatic camera movement. Whenever the face is visible: • use side-front or front-three-quarter angles • keep the face large enough in frame • keep the face sharp • keep head movement very slow • preserve natural facial asymmetry • preserve real skin texture Do not use: fast head turns, extreme profile rotation, 360 orbit, whip pan, heavy motion blur on face, exaggerated expression, face hidden behind tree or plants. ================================================== BODY CONSISTENCY RULES ================================================== Maintain the exact same: height, shoulder width, torso proportions, arm length, leg length, body size, walking posture. Do not make the subject taller, shorter, thinner, heavier or more muscular. ================================================== MOTION RULES ================================================== Use only realistic subtle motion: natural walking, subtle breathing, very small head movement, gentle standing pose, natural clothing movement, soft leaf and plant movement. No exaggerated acting. No dramatic gestures. No body distortion. ================================================== CAMERA RULES ================================================== Use smooth professional cinematic camera movement only: slow follow shot, gentle push-in, subtle side move, stable medium shot, soft final close composition. No shaky handheld effect. No fast zoom. No random angle jumps. No fisheye distortion. ================================================== FINAL QUALITY ================================================== Ultra-realistic live-action quality, authentic skin texture, realistic hair, correct anatomy, natural clothing folds, realistic outdoor lighting, premium shallow depth of field, clean cinematic 4K outdoor look. ================================================== NEGATIVE PROMPT ================================================== No face change, no identity drift, no face morphing, no face swap, no beautification, no skin smoothing, no hairstyle change, no age change, no height change, no body change, no outfit change, no extra people, no duplicate subject, no broken hands, no extra fingers, no distorted body, no cartoon, no anime, no painting, no cheap CGI, no text, no logo, no watermark.
