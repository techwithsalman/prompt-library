Use the uploaded male photo as the ONLY identity reference for the male. Preserve his exact face, hairstyle, body, height, proportions, skin tone and outfit. No face change, no body change, no outfit change. Create a 14-second ultra-realistic vertical 9:16 Bollywood luxury music-video scene at NIGHT. A metallic bright-green luxury sports car is parked in the same position throughout. The male is already leaning back against the car from the FIRST FRAME. ONE fictional ADULT female is extremely close in front of him. She is naturally leaning back against the male while he remains supported by the car. Keep the exact close layered composition: CAR → MALE → FEMALE. The female remains close to the male and car for the entire shot. The male gently holds her at the waist/side. She makes the same subtle playful movement as a real Bollywood music-video performance and briefly moves his hand away. She then remains close and naturally adjusts her hair. IMPORTANT: NO TALKING. NO SPEAKING. NO LIP-SYNC. NO CONVERSATION. NO WALKING. NO DANCING. NO SEPARATING. NO EXTRA ACTIONS. Do not invent any new movement. Both characters should perform only subtle natural movements while maintaining the same close pose. The male stays leaning against the SAME green car throughout. The female stays directly in front of him throughout. Nighttime luxury city background with illuminated buildings, warm city lights, deep cinematic shadows and soft bokeh. Camera slowly pushes in and makes a subtle sideways movement while keeping the same characters, car and background. Real adult human appearance, natural skin texture, natural blinking, breathing, realistic hands, realistic hair and clothing movement, realistic shadows and car reflections. Premium Bollywood music-video cinematography, photorealistic 4K, cinematic depth of field, smooth slow motion. FINAL LOCK: SAME MALE FACE + SAME MALE BODY + SAME OUTFIT + SAME FEMALE + SAME FEMALE OUTFIT + SAME GREEN CAR + SAME NIGHT BACKGROUND + SAME POSE. NEGATIVE: talking, speaking, lip movement, dialogue, walking, dancing, standing separately, changing pose, moving away from car, different car, different background, face change, body change, outfit change, identity drift, body morphing, extra people, duplicate characters, distorted hands, extra fingers, cartoon, CGI, plastic skin, AI look, camera jump, scene change, flickering, blurry frames.

What Is a Romantic Couple AI Video Editing Prompt

A romantic couple AI video editing prompt is a text instruction you give to an AI video tool. It tells the AI how to turn a couple’s photos into a short, emotional video. The prompt controls the mood, camera movement, lighting, transitions, and music style of the final video.

Instead of manually editing clips in software like Premiere Pro or CapCut, you describe the exact look you want. The AI tool then generates the video based on your description.

How to Use This Prompt

  1. Choose two to five clear couple photos with good lighting.
  2. Open an AI video tool that supports image-to-video generation.
  3. Upload your photos in the order you want them to appear.
  4. Paste the prompt above into the text box.
  5. Replace the background description with details that match your actual photos.
  6. Generate the video and review the result.
  7. Regenerate with small prompt changes if the mood or movement does not feel right.

Tips to Get Better Results

Use photos where both faces are clearly visible. AI tools struggle with blurry or dark images, so start with the best quality photos you have.

Keep your background description specific. Instead of writing “outdoor,” write “quiet park with autumn leaves” or “rooftop at night with city lights.” Specific details help the AI generate a more accurate scene.

Test one photo first before generating a full sequence. This saves credits and time if the tool interprets your prompt differently than expected.

Match the music mood to the visual style. A soft instrumental track works better than an upbeat song for this romantic, cinematic style.

Best Tools for This Prompt

This prompt works with most modern AI video generation tools that accept image inputs, including tools built on image-to-video models. Results vary by platform, so it is worth testing the same prompt across two or three tools to compare output quality before settling on one.

FAQ

Can I use this prompt with any AI video tool? Most image-to-video AI tools will work, but results depend on the specific model. Some tools follow camera movement instructions more accurately than others.

How many photos should I use for one video? Two to five photos work best. Using too many photos in one prompt can confuse the AI and reduce output quality.

Can I change the mood from romantic to something else? Yes. Change the mood and lighting keywords in the prompt to match a different style, such as playful, adventurous, or formal.

Do I need professional photos for this to work well? No, but clear and well-lit photos give noticeably better results than dark or blurry ones.

Is this prompt free to use? Yes, you can copy and use this prompt with any AI video tool of your choice.
