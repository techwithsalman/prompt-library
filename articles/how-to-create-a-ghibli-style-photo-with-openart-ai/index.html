How to Create a Ghibli-Style Photo with OpenArt AI

By Prompt Library • 31 March 2025

Hey there! If you’ve been scrolling through social media lately, you’ve probably seen those dreamy Ghibli-style photos popping up everywhere. You know, the ones with soft colors, whimsical vibes, and that magical Studio Ghibli feel?

Good news—you can make your own, and it’s super easy! All you need is a free AI tool called OpenArt. Let’s walk through the steps together so you can create your own Ghibli masterpiece in no time.

Step 1: Visit the OpenArt Website

First things first, open your browser and head to https://openart.ai. This is where the magic happens! It’s a free tool that lets you turn regular photos into Ghibli-style art. No fancy skills needed—just a few clicks.

OpenArt AI

Step 2: Find the “Start Converting Now” Button

Once you’re on the OpenArt homepage, look for the “Start Converting Now” button. It’s usually pretty easy to spot. Click it to get started. This takes you to the part of the site where you can upload your photo and transform it.

Step 3: Sign Up for Free

Before you can start creating, you’ll need an account. Don’t worry—it’s free! After clicking “Start Converting Now,” you’ll see a “Sign Up for Free” button. Click that, then fill in your email and a password. It takes like 30 seconds, and you’re in!

Step 4: Upload Your Photo

Now that you’re signed up, it’s time to pick a photo. Choose one from your phone or computer—maybe a selfie, a pet pic, or a cool landscape. Upload it by dragging it into the box or clicking to browse your files. Easy peasy.

Step 5: Choose the Ghibli Style

Here’s the fun part! OpenArt has different style options, and you want that classic Studio Ghibli look. Look for a filter or setting labeled “Ghibli” or “Studio Ghibli.” Select it, and the AI will start working its magic on your photo.

Step 6: Create Your Ghibli Image

Once you’ve picked the Ghibli style, hit the “Generate” or “Create” button (it might say something similar). Give it a few seconds—the AI needs a moment to turn your photo into a whimsical, hand-drawn-looking masterpiece. You’ll see the result right on your screen.

Step 7: Download and Share

Love what you see? Awesome! There’s usually a “Download” button—click it to save your new Ghibli-style photo to your device. Now you can share it with your friends on Instagram, X, or wherever you hang out online. Show off your creativity!

Bonus Tips

  • Pick a clear photo: The better the original, the cooler the Ghibli version.
  • Experiment: Try different pics to see what looks best in that Ghibli vibe.
  • Have fun: This is all about playing around and enjoying the process!

And that’s it! In just a few minutes, you’ve gone from a regular photo to a Ghibli-style work of art—all for free with OpenArt. It’s such a cool way to bring a little Studio Ghibli magic into your life. So, what are you waiting for? Grab a photo, head to https://openart.ai, and start creating.
