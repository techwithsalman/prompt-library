Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli CapCut Template: Complete Guide to Use This Trending Reel

By Prompt Library • 26 August 2026

“Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli” has become one of the most used sounds on Instagram and CapCut in recent weeks.

If you have seen this audio playing under aesthetic reels, couple videos, and glow-up transitions, you already know why creators are rushing to grab this template.

In this article, I will explain what this CapCut template is, why it is trending, and how you can use it step by step to create your own reel.

What Is the Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli CapCut Template

The Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli template is a pre-made CapCut project built around this popular song. It comes with the timing, transitions, text animations, and effects already set. All you need to do is add your own photos or video clips, and the template automatically arranges everything to match the beat of the song.

This is what makes CapCut templates so useful. You do not need editing skills or hours of free time. The hard work of syncing clips to music has already been done by the original creator. You just drop in your content and export.

Why This Song and Template Are Trending

A few reasons are driving the popularity of this particular template right now.

First, the song itself has a soft and emotional tone that works well for slow-motion aesthetic edits. It fits couple reels, friendship reels, and nostalgic throwback posts equally well.

Second, the template’s transitions are smooth and beginner friendly. Even users who have never edited a reel before can get a polished, professional-looking result on the first try.

Third, trends on Instagram move fast, and once a few large creators use a sound, smaller creators follow to ride the same wave of reach. This song crossed that tipping point recently, which is why you are seeing it everywhere on your feed.

How to Find and Use the Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli CapCut Template

Follow these steps to use the template on your phone.

Step 1: Open the CapCut app
Make sure you have the latest version of CapCut installed from the Play Store or App Store. Templates are updated frequently, and an outdated app can cause the template to not load properly.

Step 2: Search for the template
Tap on the Templates tab inside the app and search “Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli” in the search bar. You will see a few versions of the template made by different creators. Pick the one with the layout and transition style you like best.

Step 3: Tap Use Template
Once you open the template preview, tap the “Use Template” button. CapCut will ask you to select photos or videos from your gallery to fill the slots.

Step 4: Add your photos or clips
Select images or video clips in the order you want them to appear. Try to match bright, clear shots to the slots that fall on strong beats of the song, since this is where the transition looks most impactful.

Step 5: Preview and adjust
Play the preview once your clips are added. If any transition feels off, you can trim or reposition individual clips by tapping on them in the timeline.

Step 6: Export in high quality
Go to export settings and choose 1080p resolution with 30 or 60 fps. This keeps the video sharp when you upload it to Instagram or YouTube Shorts.

Tips to Make Your Reel Stand Out

A template gives you the structure, but small choices still make a big difference in how your final reel looks.

Use good lighting in your photos and clips. Even the best template cannot fix a dark or blurry image.

Keep a consistent color tone across all your clips. If your photos have very different lighting or filters, the transitions will look mismatched.

Add a simple caption that connects to the mood of the song. Since this track has an emotional and nostalgic feel, captions about friendship, memories, or relationships tend to perform well.

Post at a time when your audience is most active. Even a well-made reel gets buried if it is posted when your followers are offline.

Common Problems and How to Fix Them

Some users face small issues while using CapCut templates. Here are quick fixes for the most common ones.

If the template does not load, check your internet connection and update the CapCut app to the latest version.

If clips appear stretched or cropped oddly, try selecting photos and videos that are closer to a 9:16 vertical ratio before adding them to the template.

If the audio is out of sync after export, avoid trimming the original template timing manually unless you are confident about frame-level adjustments.

Is It Free to Use This Template

Yes, CapCut templates including this one are free to use for personal content. You do not need a CapCut Pro subscription to access the basic version of the Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli template. Some advanced effects inside certain versions may be marked as Pro features, but the core template works without any payment.

Final Thoughts

The Teri Koyal Jaisi Boli CapCut template is a simple way to create an aesthetic, beat-synced reel without spending hours on manual editing. With just a few personal photos or clips, you can put together a video that matches the current trend on Instagram and Reels. Follow the steps above, keep your visuals clean and well lit, and your reel should be ready to post in a few minutes.
