KKR vs GT Dream11 Team Prediction: IPL 2025 Match 39 – April 21, 2025

By Prompt Library • 21 April 2025

Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) face Gujarat Titans (GT) at Eden Gardens in Kolkata in Match 39 of IPL 2025. GT currently leads the points table with 5 wins from 7 matches, while KKR sits mid-table with 3 wins from 7 matches. This match is crucial for both teams as they look to improve their standings.

Team News

Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR)

  • Recent Form: Inconsistent with 3 wins from 7 matches
  • Notable Performance: Recent collapse against Punjab Kings (bowled out for 95 while chasing 112)
  • Key Players: Sunil Narine (all-rounder), Quinton de Kock (aggressive opener), Andre Russell (game-changer), Varun Chakaravarthy (spinner)

Gujarat Titans (GT)

  • Recent Form: Excellent with 5 wins from 7 matches
  • Notable Performance: Recent victory over Delhi Capitals
  • Key Players: Sai Sudharsan (365 runs at avg 52), Shubman Gill (captain), Prasidh Krishna (14 wickets in 7 matches), Rashid Khan (spinner)

Probable Playing XIs

Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR)

Gujarat Titans (GT)

Head-to-Head Record

  • KKR and GT have faced each other only 3 times
  • GT leads with 2 wins vs KKR’s 1 win
  • Their last meeting in IPL 2024 was abandoned due to rain

Venue and Conditions

  • Venue: Eden Gardens, Kolkata
  • Pitch: Batsman-friendly with true bounce and carry
  • Average First Innings Score: 170-180
  • Weather: Clear, no chance of rain

Match Prediction

While KKR has home advantage, GT’s consistent form gives them the edge. However, strong performances from KKR’s all-rounders could lead to an upset.

Key Players to Watch

  • Sai Sudharsan (GT): Four half-centuries this season
