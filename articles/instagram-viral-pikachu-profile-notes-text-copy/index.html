What Is the Instagram Pikachu Notes Trend?

Instagram has a feature called Notes. It is a small text box that appears right above profile pictures when someone opens their DM inbox.

You can write a short message there that your followers can see.Recently, people discovered something creative. By typing special emoji and symbol combinations inside this Notes section, you can make it look like a Pokémon character’s face.

Pikachu became the most popular one because almost everyone recognizes it instantly.The trend spread super fast because it is simple, fun, and looks really cool when your friends open their inbox and see a little Pikachu staring back at them.
