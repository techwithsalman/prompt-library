GT Key Players

  • Sai Sudharsan: The young batsman has been phenomenal this season with consistent performances and a strike rate of 151.
  • Prasidh Krishna: The pacer has been exceptional with the ball, picking up wickets consistently in all phases of the game.

Dream11 Strategy Tips

  1. Balance Your Team: Include players from both sides as the match could go either way.
