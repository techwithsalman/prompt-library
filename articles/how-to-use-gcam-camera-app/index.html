How to Use GCAM Camera APP? Best Tricks and Tips gcem

By Prompt Library • 15 August 2025

Google Camera (GCem) is a powerhouse of photographic innovation, packed with features that can transform your mobile photography experience. Whether you’re a seasoned pixel user or a newcomer to the ecosystem, this comprehensive guide will unlock the full potential of your smartphone camera.

1. Night Sight Magic for Morning Photos

Contrary to popular belief, Night Sight isn’t just for low-light situations. When used during morning photography, it can dramatically enhance image details. By activating Night Sight, you’ll capture more nuanced textures and improve overall image clarity, especially in subtle lighting conditions.

2. Smart Portrait Processing

Forget taking portraits directly in the camera app. Instead, capture a standard photo and leverage Google Photos’ advanced editing tools. Navigate to the edit section and use the portrait blur option under “Suggestions” or “Tools”. This approach provides superior subject isolation and more natural-looking background blur.

3. Dual Exposure: A Creative Powerhouse

GCam offers a unique dual exposure feature with separate sliders for highlights and shadows. This gives you unprecedented control over image lighting and mood. Experiment with these sliders to create dramatically different looks from the same shot, adding a professional touch to your photography.

4. Moonshot Mastery

Capturing the moon requires some technique. Zoom in completely, focus carefully, and use dual exposure by pulling down highlight and shadow sliders. If the initial shot appears blurry, don’t worry – Google’s on-blur effect can rescue your image. Pro tip: Use a tripod for the sharpest results.

5. Flexible Timer Hack

GCam’s built-in timer is limited to 3 or 10 seconds, but there’s a workaround. Use Google Assistant to trigger a photo after a custom time duration. Bonus: This method keeps Night Sight fully functional, unlike the standard camera timer.

6. Astrophotography Time-Lapse

Hidden in advanced settings, the one-second time-lapse mode is perfect for astrophotography. It might even help you capture something extraordinary – like a meteor or, who knows, an alien passing by!

7. Macro Photography Extended

On the Pixel 7 Pro, combine the macro feature with 5x extra zoom and Super Res technology to explore microscopic details. This combination reveals textures and intricacies invisible to the naked eye.

8. Macro Video Capabilities

Introduced in the June 2023 feature drop, macro video is a game-changer. For best results, use a tripod to minimize shake and capture incredibly detailed close-up motion.

9. Audio Zoom Enhancement

Activate Audio Zoom in settings to boost sound quality when zooming. It intelligently increases microphone gain, making distant subjects sound clearer by reducing background noise.

10. Night Sight Exposure Management

When taking handheld night photos, avoid maxing out exposure time. This reduces the chance of blur and ensures sharper images.

Hidden Features You Might Not Know

1. Air Gesture Photography

With timer activated, raise your palm to trigger photo capture. A subtle timer light helps you know when to remove your hand.

2. Focus Lock Technique

Tap and hold the viewfinder to lock focus. This prevents focus shifting when you or your subject move, offering manual focus-like control.

3. Subject Tracking

Simply tap on a moving subject, and the camera will continuously keep it in focus – perfect for dynamic scenes.

4. Secure Photo Storage

Quickly save photos directly to a locked folder by long-pressing the recent photo thumbnail. These won’t be cloud-backed, ensuring privacy.

5. Quick Zoom Slider

Long-press the lens switcher to instantly activate the zoom slider, allowing rapid zoom adjustments.

6. Enhanced Zoom Positioning

On Pixel 7 Pro, when zoom exceeds 15x, a floating viewfinder helps precise positioning.

7. Instant Video Recording

Long-press the shutter button to quickly start video recording. Swipe up/down to zoom or left to lock video mode.

8. Automatic Motion Unblur

A hidden gem called “Face Unblur” automatically sharpens moving subjects without manual intervention.

9. Google Lens Integration

Enable Google Lens suggestions to scan QR codes directly in the camera and get instant document scanning suggestions.

10. Smart Double-Tap Zoom

Double-tap the viewfinder for intelligent zoom that adapts based on frame content.

Essential Tips for New Users

1. Rapid Camera Launch

Double-press the power button and any volume key for instant camera access.

2. Quick Camera Flip

Use system gestures to easily switch between front and back cameras.

3. Framing Perfection

Activate framing hints for visual and haptic alignment guidance, ensuring perfectly level shots.

4. Video Stabilization

Use “Locked Mode” under video stabilization for incredibly smooth footage, even with unsteady hands.

5. Cinematic Mode

The underrated “Cinematic Pan” feature creates professionally smooth videos by recording at half-speed.

6. Motion Photography Techniques

Explore long exposure for blurring moving elements and action pan for highlighting movement.

7. Speech Enhancement

Reduce background noise in video recordings up to 4K 30fps resolution.

8. Timer Notifications

Enable “Time Light” for visual and haptic timer notifications.

9. Simultaneous Photo and Video

Capture photos while recording video without interruption.

10. Quick Social Sharing

Configure social share settings for instant sharing options after each shot.
