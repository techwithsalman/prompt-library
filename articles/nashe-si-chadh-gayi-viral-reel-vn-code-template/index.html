Nashe Si Chadh Gayi Viral Reel VN Code Template

If you scroll Instagram reels these days, you have probably seen this. A video with the song Nashe Si Chadh Gayi playing, and the edit looks smooth, cinematic, and stylish. People keep asking one question. What VN code is used in this reel?

In this post, I will explain everything about this template in simple words. No confusing terms. Just clear steps you can follow right away.

Nashe Si Chadh Gayi is a Hindi song sung by Arijit Singh. It is from the 2016 movie Befikre. The song has a smooth, romantic beat, which is why editors love using it for aesthetic reels.

The VN code template built on this song usually has slow transitions, soft zoom effects, and text that appears in sync with the music. It works well for couple reels, travel clips, and personal aesthetic posts.

Why This Reel is Going Viral

A few reasons this template is trending right now:

  • The song itself is popular and easy to recognize.
  • The transitions look professional but are actually simple to copy.
  • It works with almost any type of clip, from couple photos to daily life moments.
  • People do not need any editing skill. They just need the code.

What is a VN Code

VN is a free video editing app used on phones. When someone creates a nice edit in VN, they can save it as a project code. Anyone with that code can open it in their own VN app and get the exact same style, timing, and effects. You only need to add your own photos or video clips.

Think of it like a ready-made recipe. Someone else did the hard work of setting the timing and effects. You just add your own ingredients, which means your own pictures.

How to Use This VN Code Step by Step

Follow these steps carefully:

  1. Download the VN Video Editor app from the Play Store or App Store, if you do not have it already.
  2. Open the VN app and tap the plus icon to start a new project.
  3. Look for the option that says “Enter Code” or “Import Code” inside the app.
  4. Paste the Nashe Si Chadh Gayi VN code in that box.
  5. The template will load with the original transitions and timing.
  6. Replace the placeholder clips with your own photos or videos.
  7. Preview the reel to check the timing looks right with your clips.
  8. Export the video in high quality.
  9. Upload it to Instagram or YouTube Shorts.

That is all. No manual editing needed.

Tips to Make Your Reel Look Better

  • Use bright, clear photos. Blurry clips ruin even the best template.
  • Match the number of your photos to the number of slots in the template.
  • Keep your face or main subject in the center of the frame.
  • Add a simple caption that matches the mood of the song.
  • Post when your followers are most active, usually evening time.

FAQ

Is the VN app free to use? Yes, VN is free to download and use on both Android and iPhone.

Do I need editing experience to use this code? No. The code already has the editing done. You only add your own photos or clips.

Can I use my own song instead of Nashe Si Chadh Gayi? Yes, but the transitions are timed to match this song. Changing the song may affect the timing.

Why is my reel not matching the timing after adding photos? This usually happens when your clips are shorter or longer than the original slots. Try trimming your clips to match the template length.
