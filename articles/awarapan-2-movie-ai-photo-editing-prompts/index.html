Tips for Better Awarapan 2 AI Photo Editing

For the best Awarapan 2 AI Photo Editing results, always upload a clear and high-quality photo. A front-facing or naturally posed photograph with good lighting usually works better than a heavily blurred or low-resolution image. Try to avoid photos where the face is hidden by sunglasses, hair, shadows, or other objects. You should also keep the identity instructions in the prompt because they help the AI understand that your uploaded photo should remain the main identity reference.

Make Your Awarapan 2 Photo More Realistic

To make your Awarapan 2 AI photo look more realistic, focus on the small details. You can also experiment with different camera angles, poses, backgrounds, and lighting conditions while keeping the same identity instructions. Avoid adding too many elements to the scene because a simple and realistic composition often produces a better result.
