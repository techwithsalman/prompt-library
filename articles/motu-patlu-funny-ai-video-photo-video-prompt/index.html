Photo Prompt

? AI PROMPT
This image has a very specific AI style. The key elements are: Realistic full-body human subject in the center. Two realistic 3D cartoon characters (Motu & Patlu style) hanging from the person's hands by their shirts. Sunset golden-hour lighting. Empty rural road with trees. Eye-level camera. Natural shadows matching the environment. Vertical 9:16 composition. Social-media reel aesthetic. Bold Hindi caption at the top. Use this master prompt (replace the person description as needed): --- Prompt > Create an ultra-realistic DSLR-style full-body portrait of [PERSON DESCRIPTION] standing confidently in the middle of a quiet rural road during a beautiful golden sunset. The person is wearing [OUTFIT], looking directly at the camera with a calm, slightly smiling expression. Their face, hairstyle, skin tone, body shape, clothing, and identity must remain exactly the same as the reference image. The person is holding two realistic 3D cartoon characters suspended in the air by gripping the tops of their shirts with each hand. The left side features a chubby bald cartoon man with a black moustache wearing a red outfit and yellow leggings, hanging naturally with realistic gravity. The right side features a thin bald cartoon man with round glasses wearing a yellow kurta and orange pants, also hanging naturally. Blend both cartoon characters seamlessly into the real environment with accurate scale, realistic lighting, matching shadows, contact shadows, ambient occlusion, natural perspective, and lifelike textures so they appear physically present in the photograph instead of pasted. Background: empty countryside road, green bushes, trees, warm orange sunset sky with dramatic clouds. Camera: Canon EOS R5, 85mm lens, f/1.8, HDR photography, ultra-realistic skin texture, cinematic color grading, soft golden-hour light, shallow depth of field, realistic reflections, natural skin tones, highly detailed fabric textures, 8K resolution. Composition: centered subject, full body visible, vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel format. Add a bold white Hindi caption with black outline at the top of the image in viral reel style. Negative Prompt: low quality, blurry, AI artifacts, floating objects, incorrect anatomy, extra fingers, duplicate limbs, distorted face, cartoonish human skin, unrealistic lighting, poor shadows, oversaturated colors, watermark, logo, text errors, cropped body, bad proportions. --- If you're generating this with ChatGPT Images, Gemini, or Flux, replace [PERSON DESCRIPTION] and [OUTFIT] with the new person's details while keeping the rest of the prompt unchanged. This will recreate the same viral style with different people.
