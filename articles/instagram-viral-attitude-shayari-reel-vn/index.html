Why This Template Is Everywhere Right Now

Creators like this format because it does not need a big production setup. A single clip of you walking away, looking out of a window, or sitting alone at night is enough. The VN code handles the rest through color correction, zoom transitions, and text animation.

What Is a VN Code

For anyone new to this, VN Editor is a free mobile video editing app used widely for Instagram reels and YouTube Shorts. A VN code, sometimes called a draft code, is a shareable code that lets you import someone else’s entire editing project directly into your own VN app. This includes the layers, transitions, filters, text styles, and timing that were used in the original edit.

Instead of manually recreating every effect, you paste the code into VN, and the whole template loads with your own media in place of the original clips. This is why VN codes have become so popular for trends like this attitude shayari format. They save hours of editing work.

What Makes The Edit Look So Sharp

A few small details separate this template from a basic reel, and they are worth paying attention to if you want your version to match the original quality.

Color grading: The template uses a slightly desaturated, cool-toned filter. This gives the footage a moody, cinematic feel instead of the flat, bright look of a regular phone video.

Text timing: The shayari does not appear all at once. Each phrase fades or slides in with the beat of the audio, which keeps viewers watching until the line finishes.

Slow motion cuts: Even a two or three second clip feels longer because of subtle slow motion applied at key points, usually right when the emotional line hits.

Zoom transitions: Instead of hard cuts, the template uses soft zoom-ins between clips, which keeps the video feeling smooth rather than choppy.

Tips To Make Your Version Stand Out

Even though everyone is using the same base template, small changes can make your reel feel more personal.

Choose clips with good natural lighting. The color grading in this template works best on footage that already has decent contrast, so avoid using very dark or overexposed clips.

Keep your background simple. Busy backgrounds compete with the text overlay and reduce the impact of the shayari line.

Match your expression to the mood. Since this is an attitude-driven template, a calm, composed expression usually works better than an overly dramatic one.

Add your own caption on Instagram instead of copying others word for word. A caption that reflects your own situation will connect more with your followers.
