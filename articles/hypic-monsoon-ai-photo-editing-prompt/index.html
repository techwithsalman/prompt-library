What Is AI Expand?

To get more out of your images, many people are now using the AI Expand tool. It’s a feature that lets you extend your photo beyond the original crop. But here’s the twist — most tools only allow basic expansion. They don’t let you add customized elements in the expanded area.

That’s where Hypic stands out. It gives you control over what you want to include in the expanded section. So if you want to add rain, flowers, or even change the background to a waterfall scene — you can do it by just entering the right prompt.

If you’re looking for a good tool, just search AI Expand Tool on Google — there are many free ones available.

How To Use Hypic Monsoon AI Photo Editing Prompt

Here’s a step-by-step guide to using Hypic and applying the monsoon prompts:

  1. Install the Hypic App – Open it and upload your photo.
  2. Go to Adjust Section – You’ll see several options here.
  3. Select Crop Option – Crop the photo to fit the area you want to edit or expand.
  4. Choose Expand Tool – Once you enter this tool, it will ask for a prompt.
  5. Paste the Prompt – Choose any of the prompts shared above.
  6. Set the Size – Adjust the image size and area you want to generate.
  7. Generate Your Image – Hit the generate button and watch the magic happen!

In just a few clicks, your photo will be transformed into a beautiful monsoon-themed image. You’ll be surprised how real it looks.
