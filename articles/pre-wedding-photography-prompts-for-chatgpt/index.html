How to Use These Pre Wedding Prompts

  1. Open ChatGPT, Gemini Nano Banana or any AI image tool you already use.
  2. Upload a clear photo of both faces in good light — this decides how well the couple’s faces match.
  3. Copy any prompt above and paste it along with your photo.
  4. In the countdown prompts, change the number of days and the two names to your own before generating.
  5. If the faces change, add “do not change the faces, keep 100% same as uploaded photo” and try again.

You can also swap the outfit colours, the location or the time of day in any prompt to get a completely different pre-wedding shoot from the same structure.
