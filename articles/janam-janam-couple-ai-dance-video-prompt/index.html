Generate Video

With the right prompt and reference images, you can create a romantic, cinematic dance sequence featuring two people while keeping their appearance consistent throughout different scenes.

In this tutorial, you’ll learn how to use our Couple AI Dance Video Prompt in Flow AI and create a stylish vertical video that can be used for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, or other social media platforms.

What Is a Couple AI Dance Video?

A couple AI dance video is an AI-generated video where two people appear together in a cinematic dance performance. Instead of filming the entire performance with a camera, you provide reference photos and a detailed text prompt describing the characters, location, clothing, movements, camera angles, and overall visual style.

The AI then uses those instructions to generate the video.

Our prompt is designed around a cinematic dance performance on a circular wooden stage. It includes dramatic lighting, slow-motion movements, expressive dancing, spins, dips, and a final emotional embrace.

The result is designed to look like a premium cinematic performance rather than a basic AI animation.

What You Need Before Starting

Before generating your video, prepare the following:

  • One clear reference photo of the man
  • One clear reference photo of the woman
  • The Couple AI Dance Video Prompt
  • Access to Flow AI
  • A little patience for generating and refining the video

For the best results, choose reference photos where the faces are clearly visible and the lighting is reasonably good.

Avoid heavily edited photos, extreme angles, or images where the face is partially hidden.

How to Create a Couple AI Video in Flow AI

Step 1: Prepare Your Reference Photos

Start by selecting two photos.

The first photo should represent the male character, while the second should represent the female character.

Use photos that clearly show each person’s face. Since the prompt asks the AI to maintain character consistency, good reference images can help the model understand the appearance of each person.

You can use your own photos or photos of people who have given you permission to use their likeness.

Step 2: Open Flow AI

Open Flow and start a new video generation project.

Depending on the version of Flow you are using, the interface and available generation options may change over time. Look for the option that allows you to create a video using text instructions and reference images.

Upload the two reference photos when the interface provides the option.

The goal is to tell the AI which image represents each character so it can maintain their appearance during the video.

Step 3: Add the Couple AI Dance Prompt

Now copy the complete prompt from this article and paste it into the video generation prompt field.

The prompt contains detailed instructions about the characters, outfits, environment, lighting, camera movements, and seven different scenes.

One of the most important parts is the character-consistency instruction.

It tells the AI to maintain the same facial identity, appearance, body type, hairstyle, and clothing throughout the sequence.

However, keep in mind that AI video models can still produce small changes between frames. A detailed prompt can improve consistency, but no prompt can guarantee perfect identity preservation in every generated frame.

Understanding the 7 Scenes

The prompt is divided into seven scenes to create a complete visual story.

Scene 1 – Entering the Stage

The couple walks onto the center of a circular wooden stage while holding hands.

A single spotlight illuminates them against a dark background. The slow movement creates an intimate opening and establishes the cinematic atmosphere.

Scene 2 – Looking at Each Other

The second scene moves closer to the couple.

They face each other while holding hands and looking into each other’s eyes. This gives the video an emotional and personal feeling.

Scene 3 – Joyful Movement

The couple raises their joined hands and smiles.

This scene introduces more energy while maintaining the graceful dance style.

Scene 4 – 360-Degree Spin

This is one of the most dynamic parts of the prompt.

The man holds the woman and performs a smooth 360-degree rotation while carrying her. Her long hair, coat, and scarf move naturally with the motion.

The camera also moves around them to emphasize the full rotation.

Scene 5 – Open Dance Pose

The couple spreads their arms while holding hands and leans backward slightly.

The spotlight and dark background create a dramatic stage-performance look.

Scene 6 – Classic Dance Dip

The man gently supports the woman as she leans backward into a classic dance dip.

Her hair falls toward the floor while the coat creates additional movement. He then brings her smoothly back to an upright position.

Scene 7 – Emotional Ending

The final scene creates the emotional conclusion.

The man lifts the woman and rotates while carrying her before gently lowering her into a close embrace.

The camera slowly zooms out while the spotlight remains around them, giving the video a cinematic ending.

Why the Prompt Uses Detailed Instructions

A common mistake when generating AI videos is using a very short prompt such as:

“Create a romantic couple dancing together.”

Although this describes the basic idea, it doesn’t tell the AI enough about the scene.

Our prompt provides instructions for:

  • Character appearance
  • Clothing
  • Facial consistency
  • Stage design
  • Lighting
  • Camera movement
  • Dance movements
  • Hair and fabric motion
  • Color grading
  • Slow-motion movement
  • Scene transitions
  • Video aspect ratio
  • Watermark placement

This additional information gives the AI a much clearer creative direction.

Recommended Video Settings

For Instagram Reels, Shorts, and similar platforms, a 9:16 vertical format is a good choice.

The prompt is designed around a cinematic vertical composition, allowing the full bodies of both characters to remain visible during the dance.

If Flow provides options for quality, choose the highest quality available to you.

You can also experiment with different generation settings if the first result doesn’t perfectly follow the choreography.

How to Improve AI Video Results

Don’t worry if your first generation isn’t perfect.

AI video generation can sometimes struggle with complicated movements such as lifts, spins, hands, feet, or fast hair movement.

If a scene looks unnatural, try regenerating it with simpler movement instructions.

For example, if the 360-degree lift creates unwanted motion, you can generate that particular scene again with a slower rotation and clearer camera instructions.

Using consistent reference images and keeping the clothing description unchanged can also help maintain character continuity.

Adding the NoorEditing07 Watermark

The prompt includes a small watermark reading “NoorEditing07”.

It is instructed to remain in the same bottom corner throughout the video with low opacity and a minimal appearance.

If your video generator doesn’t render text accurately, you can add the watermark afterward using a video editing application. This can often provide cleaner and more consistent results.

Final Thoughts

Creating a cinematic couple AI dance video with Flow AI can be a fun way to turn ordinary reference photos into a creative visual story.

The key is using a detailed prompt that clearly explains the characters, environment, choreography, camera movement, lighting, and visual style.

Our seven-scene Couple AI Dance Video Prompt is designed to provide that complete direction, from the couple entering the stage to the final emotional embrace.

For the best results, use clear reference photos, carefully follow the instructions in Flow, and regenerate individual scenes when the AI struggles with complex movements.

You can copy the full prompt above and experiment with different reference photos, outfits, stage environments, and cinematic styles to create your own unique couple AI video.

Tip: Always use photos and likenesses that you own or have permission to use, especially if you plan to publish the resulting video publicly.
