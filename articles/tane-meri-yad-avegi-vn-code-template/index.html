Tane Meri Yad Avegi VN Code Template

By Prompt Library • 16 August 2026

Remember that line from Barsaat that keeps playing on every second reel? Banjaare’s “Tanne Meri Yaad Aavegi” has become one of the most used audios on Instagram and VN app right now. This template combines a glitch color shift, floating neon stars, and Hindi calligraphy text that reads “Aey Pyaar”, instantly giving any simple photo an aesthetic look.

Why this template is trending

This clip is only a few seconds long, which makes it a perfect fit for both reels and stories. The glitch effect combined with sparkle stars creates a dreamy, nostalgic mood that pairs well with breakup or missing-someone captions. That is why close-up photos are getting the best response with this template.

How to create the video using the VN code

First, open the VN app on your phone. If it is not installed, download it before starting. Then scan the QR code shown above or tap the “Use On VN App” button. This opens the template directly inside VN, with the audio and effects already set up.

Next, add your own photo or video into the template. Tap on the placeholder clip, choose the replace option, and select your photo from the gallery. Make sure your face is aligned between the two sparkle stars, since this is what makes the effect look natural.

Once everything is set, check the preview before exporting. If the timing looks right, export the video and make sure to select HD quality.

Tips for the best result

A front-facing photo with good lighting always gives a better outcome. Blurry or dark photos stop the glitch effect from standing out properly. If the face position is not perfectly aligned on the first try, you can reopen the clip and adjust the position again, since VN makes this easy to fix.

FAQ

Is this template free?
Yes, this template is completely free inside the VN app, and no extra purchase is needed.

Which phones support it?
VN app works on both Android and iPhone, and this template runs smoothly even on mid-range phones.
