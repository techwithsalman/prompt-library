Create With Flow AI

The idea is simple: upload your reference photo, use a detailed prompt, and generate a sequence of cinematic scenes. The prompt is designed around a traditional grey kurta-pajama outfit, a heritage monument courtyard, natural daylight, and smooth fashion-walk movements.

In this guide, you will learn how to use the prompt step by step and how to get better results from Flow AI.

What Is the Royal Kurta Fashion Walk AI Video?

The Royal Kurta Fashion Walk AI Video is a cinematic AI video concept where a person appears in a traditional kurta-pajama outfit while walking and posing at a beautiful heritage location.

The video contains seven different scenes. Each scene uses a different camera angle, but the character, outfit, location, lighting, and overall visual style remain consistent.

The concept works especially well for:

  • Instagram Reels
  • YouTube Shorts
  • TikTok-style vertical videos
  • Fashion edits
  • Profile videos
  • Traditional outfit showcases
  • AI cinematic videos
  • Personal social media content

The most important part is the reference photo. Your uploaded photo acts as the visual reference for the person, helping the generated character resemble the person in the image.

Main Features of This AI Video Prompt

This prompt focuses on several important elements.

1. Your Reference Face

The prompt asks the AI to use the uploaded reference photo for the man’s facial appearance, hairstyle, skin tone, body build, and overall proportions.

This is useful when you want the final video to look like you rather than a completely different AI-generated person.

2. Traditional Royal Outfit

The character wears a grey kurta-pajama set with a mandarin collar and subtle silver embroidery. Matching pajama pants and black formal slip-on shoes complete the traditional appearance.

The outfit remains consistent across the scenes.

3. Heritage Background

The video takes place in an outdoor heritage monument courtyard featuring white domes, stone archways, red sandstone railing, carved jaali patterns, and greenery.

This gives the video a premium and traditional visual atmosphere.

4. Seven Cinematic Scenes

The prompt contains seven scenes with different camera compositions. These include close-ups, walking shots, side profiles, full-body shots, and medium shots.

Changing the camera angle keeps the final video visually interesting.

How to Create Your Own Royal Kurta Fashion Walk Video in Flow AI

Follow these steps carefully.

Step 1: Choose a Good Reference Photo

First, select a clear photo of yourself.

For the best identity consistency, use a photo where your face is clearly visible. Avoid heavily edited photographs, extreme filters, very dark images, or photos where your face is partially hidden.

A clear reference image gives the AI more useful visual information.

If possible, choose a photo with:

  • Clear facial visibility
  • Good lighting
  • Natural skin appearance
  • Visible hairstyle
  • Natural body proportions
  • Minimal filters
  • No major obstruction over the face

The better the reference image, the easier it is for the AI to understand your appearance.

Step 2: Open Flow AI

Open Flow AI and start a new video-generation project.

The exact interface and available options may change over time, so use the image/reference upload option available in your current version.

Look for the option that allows you to add an image or reference photo.

Step 3: Upload Your Reference Photo

Upload your selected photograph as the reference image.

The purpose of this image is to provide the visual identity for your character.

After uploading it, make sure the image is properly recognized before entering the prompt.

Do not use somebody else’s photograph without permission. For personal AI videos, it is best to use your own image or an image you have permission to use.

Step 4: Copy the Royal Kurta Prompt

Now copy the complete Royal Kurta Fashion Walk AI Video Prompt.

The prompt contains instructions about:

  • Character appearance
  • Face and hairstyle
  • Skin tone
  • Body proportions
  • Clothing
  • Location
  • Lighting
  • Camera angles
  • Seven scenes
  • Color grading
  • Video format
  • Watermark

Paste the prompt into the appropriate prompt field in Flow AI.

Step 5: Keep the Character Consistent

Character consistency is one of the most important parts of this video.

The prompt specifically tells the AI to maintain the same person throughout all seven scenes.

It also keeps the outfit and location consistent.

However, AI-generated videos can sometimes introduce small differences between scenes. A clear reference image and precise instructions can help reduce these changes, but no prompt can guarantee perfect identity preservation in every generation.

Step 6: Generate the Scenes

The prompt includes seven cinematic scenes.

Scene 1 focuses on the hands holding sunglasses while walking.

Scene 2 moves closer to the character as he walks toward the camera.

Scene 3 shows a side-profile moment while putting on the sunglasses.

Scene 4 presents a full-body fashion pose with the monument visible behind him.

Scene 5 uses a back-angle composition with the character looking over his shoulder.

Scene 6 shows a confident full-body walk toward the camera.

Scene 7 finishes with a calm medium shot against the heritage background.

Together, these scenes create the feeling of a short cinematic fashion sequence.

Step 7: Use the 9:16 Format

For Reels, Shorts, and other vertical platforms, use a 9:16 aspect ratio.

The prompt is already designed for vertical video.

A vertical composition also gives enough space to show the person’s full body in walking scenes while keeping the monument visible in the background.

Step 8: Check the Generated Video

Once Flow AI finishes generating the video, watch the complete sequence carefully.

Check whether:

  • The face remains consistent
  • The hairstyle stays similar
  • The outfit remains grey
  • The silver embroidery is visible
  • The sunglasses look natural
  • The monument remains consistent
  • The body proportions look realistic
  • The walking motion looks natural
  • The lighting remains consistent
  • The video stays vertical

If one scene looks different, regenerate that scene if your workflow allows individual scene regeneration.

Step 9: Add Final Editing

After generating the clips, you can combine them in a video editor.

Arrange the scenes in the same 1-to-7 order to create a smooth progression.

You can also add suitable background music, gentle transitions, and simple cinematic sound effects.

Avoid adding too many effects because the original prompt already aims for a clean cinematic appearance.

Step 10: Export and Share

Finally, export your completed vertical video in a suitable quality for your social media platform.

Before publishing, watch the exported version once more to make sure the face, clothing, scenes, and watermark look correct.

Why This Prompt Can Produce Better Results

A good AI video prompt should not only describe the person. It should also explain the environment, clothing, camera movement, lighting, and continuity.

This prompt does exactly that.

Instead of simply saying “create a man walking in a kurta,” it gives Flow AI much more visual information.

For example, it specifies the grey kurta, silver embroidery, heritage architecture, red sandstone jaali railing, greenery, natural daylight, cinematic color grading, and different camera shots.

These details help establish a stronger visual direction.

Tips for Better Royal Fashion Videos

Use a Natural Reference Photo

Avoid using a heavily edited selfie as your reference. Natural images generally provide better visual information.

Keep the Prompt Unchanged

If you are generating multiple scenes, avoid changing important character details between generations.

Changing the outfit, hairstyle, or location description can make continuity more difficult.

Use Consistent Lighting

The prompt uses soft natural daylight. Keeping the lighting direction consistent helps the scenes feel like they belong to the same video.

Avoid Excessive Camera Movement

Smooth camera movement usually works better for a luxury fashion-walk concept than extremely fast movements.

Review Hands and Accessories

AI video generation can occasionally produce inconsistencies in hands, sunglasses, clothing details, or accessories. Check these areas before publishing.

Final Thoughts

The Royal Kurta Fashion Walk AI Video Prompt For Flow AI is a simple way to create a premium-looking traditional fashion video using your own reference photo.

The key is to start with a clear reference image and provide Flow AI with detailed instructions about the character, clothing, environment, camera shots, and visual style.

With the seven-scene structure, you can create a complete cinematic sequence instead of relying on one repetitive shot. The grey kurta, silver embroidery, heritage architecture, greenery, and natural daylight also give the video a distinctive royal Indian aesthetic.

Remember that AI generation can sometimes produce small visual differences. If the first result is not perfect, refine or regenerate the affected scene rather than changing the entire concept.

Now, upload your own reference photo, paste the prompt into your Flow AI workflow, generate the scenes, combine them in order, and create your own Royal Kurta Fashion Walk AI Video for social media.
