How To Create Photo From Bing AI

Here are the steps to generate images from Bing AI Image Creatror :

  1. First, Copy any IPL Team Photo Prompt from above given prompts.
  2. Then, Click on above “Create Photo” Button.
  3. After that you will redirected to Bing AI website
  4. Paste prompt that you have copied.
  5. Change your name and IPL team name in prompt
  6. Click on “Create” button.
