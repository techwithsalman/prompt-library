Tips to Get Better Results

Use photos where every face is clearly visible. AI tools handle group photos better when there is no heavy shadow or blur on any face.

Keep text short. Long captions often get cut off or distorted in AI generated images, so stick to a few words for the overlay prompts.

Match the color mood to your photo’s original lighting. A photo taken indoors under yellow light works better with a warm golden prompt than a cold blue tone.

Generate two or three versions of the same prompt and pick the best one, since AI outputs can vary slightly each time.

Best Tools for These Prompts

These prompts work with most AI image editing tools that accept a photo along with a text instruction. Since output quality varies by tool, it helps to test the same prompt on two or three platforms and compare results before picking your final edit.

FAQ

Can I use these prompts with a photo of more than five friends? Yes, but results are more accurate with smaller groups. Large groups may cause some faces to look distorted.

Can I change the text in these prompts? Yes, replace the sample caption with any text you want, but keep it short for the best result.

Do these prompts work for solo photos too? Yes, most of them work fine on solo or two-person photos as well.

Which format is best for Instagram Stories? Use the vertical 9:16 format mentioned in the poster style prompt for Stories and WhatsApp status.

Are these prompts free to use? Yes, you can copy and use any of these prompts with your preferred AI photo editing tool.
