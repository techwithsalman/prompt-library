How to Create a Special Raksha Bandhan Photo (No Editing Skills Needed!)

Create Now

Creating beautiful Raksha Bandhan photos is now super easy—even if you don’t know how to edit! You just need to tell ChatGPT what kind of photo you want, and it will help create it for you. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Open your browser and search for “Salman Khan Edits”.
  2. Scroll down and look for the latest Raksha Bandhan post.
  3. Click on it and scroll again—you’ll find some ready-made prompts.
  4. Copy any prompt you like.
  5. Open the ChatGPT app (you’ll need to sign in, you can use your Google account).
  6. Upload a photo of the brother and sister.
  7. Paste the prompt into the chat and hit the arrow (or “Create”) button.
  8. Wait a few minutes—your photo will be generated.
  9. Download the final image.

How to Fix a Blurry or Mismatched Face in the Image

Many people message us saying the face in their image looks blurred or doesn’t match the real one. Here’s a quick and easy fix:

Fix Face Now
  1. Click the button or link that says “Swap your face”.
  2. No need to log in—just upload your generated photo.
  3. The website will detect the face.
  4. Now, upload your real face photo.
  5. Click the Swap button and wait a few seconds.
  6. That’s it! Your face will be replaced properly, and the issue will be solved.
