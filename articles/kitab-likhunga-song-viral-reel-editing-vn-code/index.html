Kitab Likhunga Song Viral Reel Editing VN Code

By Prompt Library • 26 March 2026

If you’ve been scrolling Instagram lately, chances are you’ve already come across those emotional, beautifully edited Reels made on the Kitab Likhunga song. The track has taken over everyone’s feeds — and for good reason.

It hits different. Whether you’re thinking about a past relationship, a crush, or someone you truly love, this song brings out feelings that most people don’t know how to put into words.

If you haven’t used VN (Video & Photo Editor) before, here’s a quick breakdown. VN is a free editing app available on both Android and iOS. It’s popular among Reel creators because it’s lightweight, beginner-friendly, and doesn’t watermark your videos.

Save the VN Code image above to your phone’s gallery, then follow the steps below to use it.

How to Make the Reel —

Step by StepFollow these steps and you’ll have your Reel ready in under 10 minutes:Download VN App

— Search for “VN Video Editor” on the Play Store or App Store and install it for free.Save the VN Code

— Download the template code image from this article and save it to your gallery.Open VN and Tap the Scanner

— On the home screen of the VN app, look for the scanner/QR icon in the top right corner and tap it.Select the Code from Gallery

— Instead of scanning with your camera, choose the option to pick the VN Code from your gallery.Download the Template

— Hit the download button once the code loads, then tap Use Template.Add Your Photos or Videos

— The template will ask you to import your clips. Select the photos/videos you want to use.Make Adjustments if Needed

— You can fine-tune clip lengths, add captions, or tweak any element before exporting.Export and Post

— Tap Export, save to your gallery, and upload directly to Instagram.That’s it. The whole process takes less time than watching the song once.
