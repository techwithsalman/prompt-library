How to Fix Face Issues for Free (No Login Required)

Swap Your Face

Many people face the issue of blurry faces or mismatched facial features when generating AI images. I’ve received a lot of comments about this problem on my Instagram reels and YouTube videos too. So, here’s the easiest way to fix it without paying or even signing up anywhere:

  1. Click on the “Swap Your Face” button below.
  2. You’ll be redirected to the official Face Swap website.
  3. Click on “Start Face Swap”.
  4. Upload your ChatGPT-generated photo in the left section and your original face photo in the right section.
  5. Tap on “Start Face Swapping”.
  6. Wait a few moments—your face will be corrected automatically.
  7. Finally, download your fixed image.
