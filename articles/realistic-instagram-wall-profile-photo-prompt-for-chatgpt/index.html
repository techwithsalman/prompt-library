Realistic Instagram Wall Profile Photo Prompt for ChatGPT

By Prompt Library • 28 July 2026

Creating unique AI photos is becoming more popular every day, and the Realistic Instagram Wall Profile Photo Prompt for ChatGPT is one of the latest viral trends. It lets you generate a realistic street-style image where a giant Instagram profile is displayed on a wall, creating a creative and eye-catching scene.

This prompt works well with ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI image generators. With the right instructions, you can create highly detailed, cinematic photos that look like they were captured with a professional DSLR camera.

Here is a detailed AI image prompt that closely recreates the uploaded image: Ultra-realistic cinematic street photography, a young Indian man viewed from behind, wearing a crisp white full-sleeve formal shirt tucked into black trousers with black formal shoes, standing on a quiet roadside with his hands clasped behind his back, looking at a giant Instagram profile mockup painted on a long old concrete wall. The oversized Instagram profile features a circular profile picture of a girl wearing a pink traditional dupatta, username "chhaviiiiii🎀🧿", "150 posts", "1 follower", "1 following", and a bio reading "sorry boys my husband🎀 is very dangor😈 so don't message me 😎💖 / my husband prince Ji🎀🧿". At the bottom of the wall is bold Hindi text: "Kya koi ladki itni loyalty ho sakti hai 🎀❤️". The wall has realistic weather stains, dirt, and black border lines, with lush green trees hanging above creating soft dappled sunlight and natural shadows on the pavement. Warm golden-hour lighting, DSLR photography, 35mm lens, f/2.8, shallow depth of field, ultra-detailed textures, realistic colors, high dynamic range, natural perspective, photorealistic, 8K, vertical composition, documentary-style photography. Negative Prompt: blurry, low quality, cartoon, CGI, painting, extra people, distorted body, duplicate objects, low resolution, watermark, logo, text artifacts, oversaturated colors, unrealistic lighting, noise, deformed hands, cropped subject.

STRICT IDENTITY LOCK: Use the uploaded person's face as the exact identity reference. Preserve 100% of the facial structure, hairstyle, thick curly hair, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, facial proportions, facial expression, and ear piercing. Do not alter the identity in any way. Ultra-realistic cinematic street photography of the same young man from the uploaded image, standing on the left side of the frame in a relaxed pose with one hand inside his trouser pocket, wearing a crisp white full-sleeve formal shirt tucked into black formal trousers with black formal shoes. The camera is positioned at a three-quarter side angle (around 45°), showing the man's left-side profile while he looks toward a giant Instagram profile mural painted on a long weathered concrete wall.
