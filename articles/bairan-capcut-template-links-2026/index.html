How to Use Bairan CapCut Template – Step by Step

Follow these steps. The whole process takes under 5 minutes.

Step 1 – Install CapCut

Download it free from the Play Store or App Store. Make sure it’s the latest version.

Step 2 – Tap the Template Link

Use the links above. The template will open directly inside the app.

Step 3 – Preview the Template

Watch the sample video. This shows you what your final video will look like.

Step 4 – Tap “Use Template”

The app will ask you to select your clips or photos from your gallery.

Step 5 – Add Your Media

Select the required number of clips. Try to match the duration shown in each placeholder.

Step 6 – Export Your Video

Tap the export button. Your video is ready. Upload it to Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts.

Pro Tips for Better Results

  • Use high-quality footage. The template looks 10x better when your clips are sharp and well-lit.
  • Use portrait-mode clips. Vertical 9:16 footage fills the frame perfectly. Landscape videos get cropped awkwardly.
  • Match the clip length. Each placeholder has a specific duration. Respect it for smooth transitions.
  • Don’t over-edit after export. The template is already polished. Extra filters can reduce quality.
  • Post during peak hours. Upload between 6–9 PM for better reach on Instagram and TikTok.
  • Use relevant hashtags. Try #CapCutTemplate #Bairan and niche-specific tags on your post.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Bairan CapCut Template free?

Yes. All CapCut templates are free to use. No paid plan needed.

Why isn’t the link opening?

Update CapCut to the latest version. Old versions sometimes can’t load new templates.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. CapCut is available on both Android and iOS. The template links work on both.

How many clips do I need?

Most Bairan templates need 3–6 clips. The app shows the exact number when you tap “Use Template.”

Can I change the music?

Yes. After importing your clips, swap the background music inside the CapCut editor before exporting.

Will my video have a watermark?

CapCut adds a small watermark by default. Tap the watermark icon before exporting to remove it. A free CapCut account login is required.
