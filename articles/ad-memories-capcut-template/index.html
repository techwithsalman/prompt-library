What Is the Sad Memories CapCut Template?

The Sad Memories template is a pre-made video editing layout inside the CapCut app. It uses a smooth clone transition effect — your old photo or video plays first, then it transitions into a new outfit clip.

This simple but emotional style is what makes it look so good. Thousands of creators on Instagram and YouTube Shorts are using it right now.

The best part? You don’t need to do any manual editing. The template does everything for you.

How to Use the Sad Memories Template – Step by Step

Follow these simple steps:

Step 1 – Download CapCut If you don’t have CapCut on your phone, download it from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iPhone). It is free.

Step 2 – Enable VPN (if needed) If the template link doesn’t open, turn on a free VPN app first. Then try the link again.

Step 3 – Tap the Template Link Click the template link above. It will open directly inside your CapCut app and show you a “Use Template” button.

Step 4 – Add Your Videos This template needs 2 video clips:

  • Clip 1 – Your old photo or video (casual look or old memory)
  • Clip 2 – Your new outfit video or current look

Select both clips in order.

Step 5 – Tap “Next” After adding your clips, tap the Next button at the bottom. CapCut will automatically edit your video using the template.

Step 6 – Export and Save Once the edit is ready, tap the Export button at the top right. Your video will save to your phone gallery.

Step 7 – Post on Instagram or YouTube Shorts Upload the video as a Reel or Short. Add a trending audio if needed.

Tips to Make Your Reel Look Even Better

  • Use a clear, well-lit old photo for Clip 1 — blurry photos ruin the effect
  • For Clip 2, record your outfit video in good lighting — natural light works best
  • If you don’t like the default song, open the video in CapCut after export and replace the audio with any trending track
  • Post your reel in the morning or evening for better reach on Instagram
